# Let one Brazilian life teach compound interest

The game can be built as a static web app that collects no data, and Brazil's 2026 economy is unusually easy to teach because its extreme numbers sit side by side. The minimum wage is **R$ 1,621 a month**. The average credit-card rotativo rate is **451.7% a year (about 15.3% a month)**. A 2024 rule stops card interest once it reaches **100% of the original debt**. And with a **13.75% Selic**, a teen's 100%-of-CDI caixinha earns roughly 8% a year above inflation. The design is riskier than the build. Brazil's best school trial of financial education (Bruhn et al., about 20,000 students) found that it raised saving but also raised the use of expensive credit. People also underestimate exponential growth and are overconfident about it. So the game has to make credit's cost felt in reais, and it has to ask players to guess before it shows them the answer. No free Brazilian game does this now. What exists is calculators, quiz games, apps for children, and 18+ trading contests. The recommended v1 keeps every interview decision: one continuous life, entry at 10, 14 or 18, monthly turns, net worth plus a ghost replay, the four mechanics, a meme-literate narrator, pt-BR data files and open licences. It adds two pacing devices that the 15-minute target forces. Standing orders let most months resolve on their own, and a fixed budget of about 60 decision stops is spread across whatever life the player has left. Legally, collecting nothing is the compliance strategy, because the audience includes under-12s and the ECA Digital has been in force since 17 March 2026. On 7 November 2026, eight candidate names were unregistered on every domain checked. The rest of this report covers the numbers the economy needs, the evidence and its warnings, the competition, a concrete v1, the stack and licences, the law, names, and a prioritised list of facts to verify before shipping.

## Brazil's 2026 numbers put a 15%-a-month card next to a 1%-a-month Selic

### Wages and the payslip: a minimum-wage worker loses only INSS and transport

The 2026 salário mínimo is **R$ 1,621/month** (R$ 7.37/hour), up 6.79% from R$ 1,518. It is indexed to the INPC plus GDP growth from two years earlier, with the real gain capped at 2.5% ([Band](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/valor-salario-minimo-2026-reajuste-r-1621-202512101209); [Genyo](https://genyo.com.br/jovem-aprendiz/)). That formula can drive the game's January wage rise. Employee INSS is progressive: **7.5% up to R$ 1,621**, then 9%, 12% and 14% up to a ceiling of R$ 8,475.55 ([Valor Final](https://valorfinal.com.br/tabela-inss-2026)). Since Lei 15.270/2025, **IRRF is zero on gross income up to R$ 5,000**, with a phase-out to R$ 7,350 ([contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/irrf-2026-como-calcular-o-imposto-de-renda-retido-na-fonte-com-a-nova-tabela-e-reducao-ate-r-7-350/)). Below that line, then, a young player's only payslip deductions are INSS and the vale-transporte share (at most 6%). At the minimum wage that means R$ 121.57 INSS and up to R$ 97.26 VT, leaving **about R$ 1,402 take-home** (computed). The employer deposits FGTS at 8% (R$ 129.68). For an apprentice it is **2%** (Lei 8.036/90 art. 15 §7) ([Senado](https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento?dm=4712943)). An MEI pays a flat DAS of R$ 82.05 to R$ 87.05 and can bill up to R$ 81,000 a year ([O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2026/01/05/mei-vai-pagar-mais-em-2026-contribuicao-mensal-sobe-para-rs-8105.html)). Sources conflict on whether LC 238/2026 raises that ceiling, so MEI belongs in v2. The 13º salário and paid férias are standard CLT, but the notes did not re-verify them, so they are on the verification list.

Income averages give the game a sense of scale. Average monthly income from all sources was **R$ 3,367 in 2025**, roughly double the minimum wage ([IBGE](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/46579-rendimento-medio-da-populacao-brasileira-atinge-r-3-367-em-2025)). Work income ran from **R$ 2,475 in the Nordeste to R$ 4,026 in the Sul**, so the Nordeste earns about 0.62× the Sudeste ([IBGE informativo](https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv102275_informativo.pdf)). That ratio is a ready-made regional multiplier for v2. Youth unemployment hit series lows: **13.8% for 18–24 in Q1 2026** and 25.1% for 14–17 ([O Imparcial](https://oimparcial.com.br/noticias/2026/06/desemprego-entre-jovens-cai-para-138-mas-baixa-remuneracao-e-rotatividade-sao-desafios/)). By education, Q2 2026 unemployment was **9.0% with ensino médio incompleto, 5.6% with superior incompleto and 3.0% with superior completo** ([Movimento Econômico/IBGE](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/trabalho/2026/08/14/pnad-nordeste-tem-quatro-dos-cinco-piores-indices-de-desocupacao-do-pais/)). These figures can serve directly as job-search odds by qualification.

### Education pays, but dropout is the norm, not the exception

The raw gap is large: graduates earn 126%, 152% or 156% more than people with only ensino médio, depending on the source ([Mackenzie](https://www.mackenzie.br/ceat/menu/noticias/arquivo/n/a/i/no-brasil-graduados-tem-renda-126-maior-que-os-trabalhadores-com-ensino-medio-aponta-pesquisa-do-ibge); [FGV CPS](https://www.cps.fgv.br/cps/bd/clippings/kc1707.pdf); [FEATA](https://feata.edu.br/2025/10/ibge-confirma-diploma-de-ensino-superior-aumenta-renda-em-ate-156/)). Causal estimates are much smaller. Finishing ensino médio is worth **+11% to +20%** ([IPEA TD 2804](https://repositorio.ipea.gov.br/bitstream/11058/11532/2/TD_2804_Sumex.pdf)), a technical occupation **+21.3% to +24.9%** ([IPEA TD 2859](https://repositorio.ipea.gov.br/bitstream/11058/11817/2/TD_2859_Sumex.pdf)), and a degree **more than 25%** ([Insper](https://repositorio-api.insper.edu.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/911733fb-8338-4659-b24c-ab57b527e581/content)). The game should keep both. Real job wages from the table above become the offers a qualified player can win. Hiring odds and dropout risk keep the *average* return closer to the causal figures, so the game does not promise every graduate an enfermeiro's piso.

Access is cheaper than most teens assume. **SISU 2026 offered about 274,800 free public places** ([DOL](https://dol.com.br/noticias/brasil/930984/sisu-2026-inscricoes-comecam-em-janeiro-e-oferecem-274-mil-vagas)). ProUni gives full scholarships at per-capita family income of up to R$ 2,431.50 and 50% scholarships up to R$ 4,863 ([Aprova Total](https://aprovatotal.com.br/prouni/)). FIES is **interest-free up to 3 minimum wages per capita**, with repayment starting 18 months after graduation and capped at 10% of gross income ([Diário do Nordeste](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/ceara/fies-2026-guia-completo-para-financiamento-estudantil-com-o-enem-1.3709118)). Private tuition has fallen in real terms for ten years: the 2026 monthly fee is **R$ 835 presencial and R$ 214 EAD** ([ABMES](https://abmes.org.br/noticias/mensalidades-da-graduacao-privada-registram-queda-real-em-2026-e-revelam-novo-ciclo-competitivo-5200)). The stake is dropout. Over 2015–2024, **58% of presencial and 63% of EAD students dropped out**, and needing to work and lacking financial aid are the main reasons ([O Povo](https://mais.opovo.com.br/jornal/reportagem/2026/05/04/alunos-da-rede-privada-e-do-ead-lideram-taxas-de-desistencias.html)). That ties career to money in the game: debt stress should raise dropout risk. One gap matters directly for an owner-named mechanic. **No SENAI, SENAC, ETEC or IF course costs were researched**, so those numbers are placeholders for now.

### Cost of living: one minimum wage does not cover a kitnet

Food is the largest unavoidable cost. In September 2026 a DIEESE basic basket cost **R$ 922.51 in São Paulo** but **R$ 663.48 in Recife**, and the national average took **50.21% of a net minimum wage** ([DIEESE](https://www.dieese.org.br/analisecestabasica/2026/202609cestabasica.pdf)). DIEESE's "salário mínimo necessário" for a family of four was R$ 7,750, or 4.78× the actual minimum. São Paulo's advertised rents averaged **R$ 65.78/m²**, so a 25 m² kitnet costs about R$ 1,650 before condo fees (computed). That is more than a whole minimum wage. National advertised rents rose **9.13% in 12 months**, about twice inflation ([Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/aluguel-sobe-o-dobro-da-inflacao-no-ano-diz-pesquisa-fipezap/)). Bus fares rose each January: São Paulo R$ 5.00→5.30, Rio R$ 4.70→5.00, Belo Horizonte R$ 5.75→6.25 ([Revista Oeste](https://revistaoeste.com/economia/capitais-comecam-2026-com-reajuste-nas-tarifas-de-transporte-publico/)). The cheapest group health plans for ages 19–23 run R$ 124–167 ([CompanyHero](https://www.companyhero.com/blog/planos-empresariais-tabela-de-precos)). For the game, this means "moving out at 18" on one minimum wage should fail in a big city unless the player shares housing or lives with family. Rents for rooms and peripheral kitnets are unsourced, so v1 should offer shared housing as a choice with a placeholder price.

The card rule the owner wants modelled comes from Lei 14.690/2023 and CMN Resolução 5.112. Since **3 January 2024**, total interest and charges on rotativo and fatura instalments **cannot exceed the original debt**: R$ 1,000 can grow to at most R$ 2,000. **IOF is excluded** from the cap. Moving from rotativo to parcelamento **does not reset** the counter, and the cap does not apply to cartão consignado ([Idec](https://idec.org.br/dicas-e-direitos/teto-de-juros-do-cartao-de-credito-conheca-mudancas-nos-limites-do-rotativo); [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/regra-que-limita-juro-do-cartao-a-100-da-divida-entra-em-vigor/)). Every fatura must show the original amount of each financed operation and the charges accrued so far. It must also carry a three-item options box: the minimum payment, the charges for the next period if only the minimum is paid, and the financing options **ranked from lowest to highest CET** ([O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-saiba-como-vai-funcionar-a-partir-de-2024.html)). Since 1 July 2024 a fatura debt can be moved free to another bank ([Seu Dinheiro](https://www.seudinheiro.com/2023/financas-pessoais/tem-dividas-no-cartao-de-credito-cmn-limita-juros-do-rotativo-a-100-e-voce-ainda-vai-poder-transferir-seus-debitos-miql)). Since 2017, rotativo has been limited to 30 days ([Idec PDF](https://idec.org.br/sites/default/files/contribuicoes_sobre_oferta_de_credito_e_endividamento_da_populacao.pdf)).

The arithmetic shows why the cap matters (computed). At 15.3% a month a balance **doubles in 4.9 months**: R$ 1,000 becomes R$ 1,153, then 1,329, 1,533, 1,767 and 2,037 by month five. The cap binds there at R$ 2,000 plus IOF. Without the cap, a year of rotativo would multiply the debt **5.5 times**. The more realistic path also teaches well. One month of rotativo followed by the fatura parcelada over 12 instalments at 9.4% a month costs **R$ 164 a month, R$ 1,968 in total**, which is R$ 968 of charges on R$ 1,000 and just under the legal ceiling. A game that let rotativo grow without limit would be factually wrong for any debt taken on since 2024.

The minimum-payment percentage and the mandatory parcelamento offer after the 30-day rotativo were **not confirmed**: the BCB FAQ would not render. Issuers now set their own minimum. For IOF, most sources give individuals **0.38% plus 0.0082% a day**, capped at 365 days ([ecarts](https://ecarts.com.br/financas/iof-2026-aliquotas-oficiais-calculo-e-como-funciona/)). One law firm lists 0.0041%, which looks like the old company rate. Both items are P0 on the verification list.

### Parcelamento is how Brazil buys, and household budgets are already 29% committed

"Parcelado sem juros" made up **42.7% of credit-card spending in Q3 2025** ([Abecs](https://uploads.finsidersbrasil.com.br/2025/11/Abecs-Apresentacao-3T25-1.pdf)), and **63.4%** of that volume was in six instalments or fewer ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/minhas-financas/pagamentos-com-cartoes-movimentaram-r-11-trilhao-no-primeiro-trimestre-de-2025/amp/)). Lei 13.455/2017 lets merchants charge less for cash or Pix than for card ([Dizer o Direito](https://www.dizerodireito.com.br/2017/06/lei-134552017-comercianteprestador-de.html)). So "sem juros" is free only when no à-vista discount is on offer. Households now commit **29.0% of monthly income to debt service** (August 2026, a series record), and household debt equals 50.1% of annual income ([O Povo/Estadão](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2026/10/29/juro-medio-no-credito-livre-sobe-a-490-em-setembro-mostra-bc-cheque-especial-vai-a-139.html)).

The BCB banned the "Pix Parcelado" brand in December 2025 ([Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2025-12/apos-adiamentos-banco-central-desiste-de-regular-pix-parcelado)). Its new rules on credit at Pix checkout start on **1 July 2027**. They require prior opt-in with a 24-hour delay and send alerts when instalments reach **40% of payment capacity**, but they set **no rate cap** ([Jornal do Comércio](https://www.jornaldocomercio.com/economia/2026/10/1268268-bc-define-regra-para-pix-no-credito-e-cria-alertas-contra-endividamento.html)). On the cost side, a CG 160 Start lists at **R$ 17,350**, and financing it over 60 months with no down payment totals **more than R$ 33,000** ([A Revista](https://arevista.com.br/consumo/honda-cg-160-start-2026-parte-de-r-17-350-veja-o-custo-financiado/)), or about R$ 550 a month. A Honda consórcio runs about R$ 320 a month over 48 months, with a 16–18% administration fee and delivery by draw ([Consulta de Placa](https://consultadeplaca.net/blog/consorcio-moto-honda-cg-160-biz-pop-2026)). Entry phones cost **R$ 849–1,700** ([TechTudo](https://www.techtudo.com.br/listas/2026/09/melhor-celular-ate-r-1500-para-comprar-em-setembro-de-2026-edqualcomprarmb.ghtml)), so a R$ 1,500 phone in 12x is R$ 125 a month, more than most mesadas. There is **no sourced 2026 example of the gap between Pix and 12x prices** at Brazilian retailers. That number sits at the core of the à-vista mechanic, so it is P0.

### Default has consequences the game can show without inventing them

**81.7 million Brazilians were negativados in February 2026**, with an average debt of **R$ 6,598** ([Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/10-anos-do-mapa-de-inadimplencia/)). People aged 18–25 are 11.45% of them, down from 15.93% in 2016 ([CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/money/macroeconomia/inadimplencia-atinge-817-milhoes-e-cresce-38-em-10-anos-no-brasil/)). That decline is partly demographic, so it does not show young people's finances getting better. The card is the main debt of **85.3% of indebted families** ([Diário do Comércio](https://dcomercio.com.br/publicacao/s/cartao-impulsiona-endividamento-recorde-das-familias-diz-cnc)). Serasa Score runs from 0 to 1000 in bands of Baixo 0–300, Regular 301–500, Bom 501–700 and Excelente 701–1000 ([Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/score/o-que-e-score/)). Serasa publishes two different factor weightings. A negative record must be removed **5 years after the due date** ([Serasa Ensina](https://www.serasa.com.br/ensina/seu-nome-limpo/divida-caduca/)). A new agreement creates a new debt ([Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/divida-prescreve-em-5-anos-mito-ou-verdade/)). Sources disagree on whether a prescribed debt can still be collected out of court. A 2023 STJ ruling said it cannot ([Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/2023-nov-27/stj-concede-perdao-ao-devedor-de-debito-prescrito//?print=1)).

The 2026 Novo Desenrola is a ready-made recovery event. It covers incomes up to 5 minimum wages and debts 90–720 days overdue, with **30–90% discounts and interest up to 1.99% a month**. Up to 20% of the FGTS balance (or R$ 1,000) can go to the creditor, and participants are **barred from betting sites for 12 months** ([Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2026-05/desenrola-20-entenda-como-usar-fgts-para-quitar-dividas); [BNLData](https://bnldata.com.br/governo-prorroga-novo-desenrola-ate-agosto-de-2026-e-mantem-proibicao-de-apostas-online/)). It closed on 31 August 2026. The game should present it as "a renegotiation programme like this sometimes exists", not as a permanent right.

### Saving and investing: 2026 real rates make fixed income look magical

Copom **held the Selic at 13.75% on 4 November 2026**, ending five 0.25-point cuts from a 15% peak ([Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/copom-mantem-selic-em-1375-e-confirma-aposta-do-mercado/); [MercoPress](https://en.mercopress.com/2026/03/18/brazil-s-central-bank-starts-easing-cycle-cuts-selic-rate-to-14.75)). CDI ran 13.65% ([guardardinheiro](https://www.guardardinheiro.com.br/quanto-rende/0/picpay)). IPCA was **4.26% in 2025** ([IBGE](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/45613-ipca-em-dezembro-vai-a-0-33-e-acumula-4-26-em-2025.html)), the 2026 Focus median is 5.22%, and the target is 3% ± 1.5 ([Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/cmn-confirma-centro-da-meta-continua-de-inflacao-a-partir-de-2025/)). That puts the real rate at **about 8% a year** (computed), among the highest anywhere.

Poupança follows a legal formula. When the Selic is above 8.5%, it pays **0.5% a month plus TR**. When the Selic is at or below 8.5%, it pays 70% of the Selic plus TR, credited on each deposit's monthly aniversário ([Clube dos Poupadores](https://clubedospoupadores.com/poupanca/rende-meio-por-cento.html)). In May 2026 that came to 0.6695% for the month and **8.32% over 12 months**, which implies a TR of about 0.17% a month, well above zero ([VRI](https://www.vriconsulting.com.br/indices/poupanca.php)). Poupança still holds **R$ 1.022 trillion** after five straight years of net withdrawals ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/economia-br/brasileiros-sacaram-r-856-bilhoes-da-poupanca-em-2025)). Pé-de-Meia deposits land in an automatic Caixa Tem poupança ([SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)), so for millions of teens it is their first account.

Tesouro Direto charges a **0.20% a year custody fee, waived on the first R$ 10,000 of Tesouro Selic** per CPF. Since 31 December 2024 the fee is charged on sale, maturity or coupon payment rather than every six months ([Tesouro Direto](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/en/b/entenda-a-nova-taxa-de-custodia); [B3](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/tipos-de-investimentos/renda-fixa/tesouro-direto/taxa-de-custodia-do-tesouro-direto-deixa-de-ser-cobrada-semestralmente/)). Minors can hold any title in their own CPF, with a guardian opening the account ([TD Família](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/b/td-familia-ensine-seus-filhos-a-investir)). Nubank's Caixinha pays 100% of CDI, or up to 120% in its "Turbo" version, and is FGC-covered up to R$ 250,000 per CPF per institution ([Adriano Freire](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/caixinha-nubank-quanto-rende-2026)). Some competitors' cofrinhos are reportedly fund-backed and not FGC-covered ([Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/mercado-pago-lanca-cofrinho-com-rendimento-de-140-do-cdi-por-prazo-limitado-e-com-regras-especificas-confira/)), which makes a good "read the fine print" card. CDB and Tesouro gains pay IR at **22.5% up to 180 days, 20% to 360, 17.5% to 720 and 15% after** ([Safra](https://oespecialista.safra.com.br/tabela-regressiva-ir-iof-renda-fixa/)). IOF eats any gain redeemed within 30 days ([Adriano Freire](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/iof-investimentos-prazos-30-dias-evitar)). Poupança and LCI/LCA remain tax-free, because MP 1303 lapsed on 8 October 2025 ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/politica/congresso-adia-votacao-de-mp-alternativa-ao-iof-para-vespera-do-prazo-de-validade/)).

Over long horizons the evidence warns against a guaranteed winner. Since 1967 the Ibovespa averaged **7.6% real (median 4.4%)** over 20-year windows, and the CDI beat it over the last 20 years ([Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/ibovespa-precisaria-subir-86-a-285-mil-pontos-para-voltar-a-mediana-calcula-gestora-rnda/)). Poupança barely beat IPCA from 2003 to 2023 and lost to it from 2013 to 2023 ([Clube dos Poupadores](https://clubedospoupadores.com/carteira-investimentos/longo.html)). The game should therefore start at 2026 rates and let the Selic drift year by year within the 2024–2026 range of about 9–15%, rather than locking in 8% real returns for 30 years.

### The under-18 money life: small mesadas, R$ 3 diamonds and a parent's card

Mesada is common and small. In 2023, 39% of parents paid one, **74% of them up to R$ 100 and 53% up to R$ 60**, mostly monthly and mostly spent on school snacks ([Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/mesada-aos-filhos/)). A 2024 wave found 56%, mostly in cash and often tied to behaviour and grades. Among non-payers, 35% said "money must be earned", 32% preferred to "give when needed" and 27% could not afford it ([CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/financas/56-das-familias-brasileiras-pagam-mesadas-para-filhos-mostra-pesquisa/)). Those answers map neatly onto four starting-family models. **21% of internet users aged 11–17 have spent money in games**, rising with age from 16% to 24% ([Cetic.br via TJGO](https://www.tjgo.jus.br/files/2026/03%20-%20Marco/Pesquisa%20TIC_Kids_online_brasil_2025.pdf)). Free Fire diamonds start at **R$ 3 by Pix** ([Free Fire Mania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/diamantes-free-fire.html)), the classic "small amounts add up" moment. The average first smartphone arrives at **10 years 3 months**, and 75% of 10–12s own one ([Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/05/09/2025/criancas-smartphone-25/)).

Minors' accounts are opened by a guardian, who sees every transaction. Nubank's covers ages 6–17 ([Estado de Minas](https://www.em.com.br/emfoco/2026/01/03/nubank-libera-conta-para-menores-de-idade-com-pix-cartao-e-controle-dos-pais-direto-no-app/)). Since September 2025, Nubank has also offered 16–17-year-olds their own **secured card whose limit equals what they keep in a Caixinha** ([Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2025/09/adeptos-ao-pix-para-troca-de-mensagens-publico-menor-de-idade-podera-ter-cartao-de-credito-proprio-no-nubank/)). Younger teens can get only an additional card on a parent's account, and its fatura lands on the parent. The Constitution bans work under 16 except as an apprentice from 14, and bans night or hazardous work under 18 ([Senado](https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento?dm=4712961)), so moto delivery is closed to minors. In practice, **1.65 million 5–17-year-olds were in child labour in 2024**, 55.5% of them aged 16–17 ([IBGE](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/pt/agencia-noticias/44541-pais-tinha-1-650-milhao-de-criancas-e-adolescentes-em-situacao-de-trabalho-infantil-em-2024.html?lang=pt-BR)).

Two government programmes shape the 14-start. Pé-de-Meia pays low-income public high-school students **up to R$ 9,200 over three years**. That is R$ 200 for enrolment and up to 9 × R$ 200 a year for attendance of at least 80%, plus **R$ 1,000 per completed year, locked until the diploma**, and R$ 200 for sitting ENEM ([SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)). Averaged over three years, that is about R$ 255 a month. Bolsa Família adds R$ 50 a month per child aged 7–18 to the family's R$ 600 floor ([Seu Crédito Digital](https://seucreditodigital.com.br/bolsa-familia-2026-extras-r-50-e-r-150/)).

The threats are documented too. **11% of 10–17-year-olds bet in 2025, 20% of boys aged 16–17** ([BNLData/Ipsos-Unico](https://bnldata.com.br/apostas-online-11-dos-jovens-brasileiros-entre-10-e-17-anos-usaram-bets-em-2025/)). 53% of 11–17 internet users saw betting promotion, and 84% saw brand content not labelled as advertising ([Cetic.br](https://cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/pt-br/20260317181001/tic_kids_online_2025_resumo_executivo.pdf.pdf)). Among Gen Z, 27% bet and **38% faced a scam in 2025** ([InfoMoney/Anbima](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=3305370)). The scams the game can use come with sources. "Free diamonds by ID" offers ask for passwords or a Pix fee ([Free Fire Mania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/diamantes-gratis-free-fire-por-id-funciona-ou-golpe.html)). Serasa says a loan that asks for money up front "is always a scam" ([Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/credito/blog/golpe-do-emprestimo-tipos-mais-comuns-e-como-se-prevenir/)). And the Receita Federal has repeatedly denied any "taxa do Pix" ([Diário de Pernambuco](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/noticia/economia/2025/01/receita-alerta-para-golpes-com-taxa-falsa-sobre-pix.html)).

## The evidence says knowledge alone can make teens bolder borrowers

Financial education works, though not dramatically. A 2022 meta-analysis of **76 RCTs covering more than 160,000 people in 33 countries** found real causal effects on knowledge and on downstream behaviours such as saving, comparable to interventions in other fields of education ([Kaiser et al., JFE](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:145:y:2022:i:2:p:255-272)). The earlier Fernandes, Lynch & Netemeyer meta-analysis found that interventions explained only **0.1% of the variance in behaviour**, with effects that were negligible after 20 months and weaker for low-income samples. The authors recommended "just-in-time" education tied to specific decisions ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2333898)). Game formats have their own evidence. An online game-based course ("Financial Escape Room") tested on 2,220 students in four countries raised financial literacy by **0.313 SD** ([Maastricht](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/the-impact-of-an-online-game-based-financial-education-course-mul/)). In the Moonshot study, strategic mechanics combined with reflection prompts raised the perceived value of the game ([MDPI](https://mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/2/227)). Bad News, a 15-minute browser game in which the player produces fake news, cut how reliable players found fake headlines by **21%**, with effects lasting up to 13 weeks when boosted ([Cambridge](https://cam.ac.uk/node/206062); [HKS](https://misinforeview.hks.harvard.edu/article/global-vaccination-badnews/)).

The most important study for this game is Brazilian. Bruhn and colleagues randomised financial education across **868 schools and about 20,000 high-school students**. Proficiency rose by about 0.25 SD, saving and budgeting improved and parents benefited too, **but students also increased their use of expensive credit** for consumer purchases ([AEJ: Applied](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aejapp/v8y2016i4p256-95.html)). The plausible mechanism is confidence. A teen who now "understands" how a card works feels safe using one. So a game that explains rotativo and leaves it feeling manageable could reproduce that effect. Exponential growth bias is the second trap. People underestimate both the rates implied by loan terms and the future value of savings. More-biased households carry more short-term debt and have lower net worth ([Stango & Zinman](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:64:y:2009:i:6:p:2807-2849)). They are also **overconfident** about their ability to do these calculations ([Levy & Tasoff](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/levymr/papers/LevyTasoff.pdf)). Brazilian teens start from a low base. PISA 2022 put Brazil's financial literacy at **416 against 498 for the OECD average, with 45% below baseline Level 2** ([OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-iv-factsheets_34d60137-en/brazil_1c815ef9-en.html)). The maths score was 379 against 472 ([Itaú Social](https://www.itausocial.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/PISA-2022-contextualizacao-e-perspectivas.pdf)). BCB's own evaluation of Aprender Valor found the largest gains among students who started lowest ([BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/cidadaniafinanceira/documentos_cidadania/serie_cidadania/S%C3%A9rie_cidadania_financeira_n9.pdf)).

These findings turn into six design rules. First, every credit decision shows its cost **in reais and in months of salary**, never only as a percentage: "esse celular vai custar 1 mês e meio do seu trampo". Second, the player predicts first. Before every investment fast-forward and before every rotativo decision, the player drags a slider to guess the balance, and the game then reveals the true figure and keeps a running "viés exponencial" score. Third, runs are short and replayable, and the ghost replay acts as the booster that Bad News found necessary. Fourth, a three-question debrief ends every run, even though the teacher kit waits for v2, because a game plus reflection is the unit the evidence supports. Fifth, the game never makes the player do formula maths: with 45% of students below baseline, it compares outcomes in money. Sixth, credit is never a power-up. It solves a cash-flow problem at a visible price, and the cheapest options (consignado, FIES, à vista) are shown next to the expensive ones, the same ranking the real fatura must display. One caveat applies throughout: **no RCT has tested an idle or incremental mechanic for teaching compound interest**, and this game's effect is unproven until it is measured.

## Brazil has simulators and quizzes, not a game about life and money

The institutional field is crowded but built to inform, not to play. The BCB's **Aprender Valor** reached more than 24,000 public schools with offline projects and simulations, and it extends to ensino médio from 2026 ([Consumidor Moderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/banco-central-educacao-financeira-jovens/); [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/financas/bc-levar-educacao-financeira-estudantes-ensino-medio-todo-pais/)). MEC's **Na Ponta do Lápis** targets 30 million students and Pé-de-Meia recipients ([MEC](https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/julho/mec-lanca-programa-de-educacao-financeira)). The rest of the field is narrow. Febraban's Bate-Bola Financeiro is quiz-gated ([Meu Bolso em Dia](https://meubolsoemdia.com.br/Materias/12-games-que-ensinam-a-poupar)). B3's HUB3 simulator and Desafio B3 are for investing and are 18+ ([Bora Investir](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/desafio-b3-bolsa-promove-competicao-de-educacao-financeira-e-investimentos/)). Tesouro Direto offers calculators. Junior Achievement's Meu Dinheiro, Meu Negócio is a mobile app ([Rio Notícias](https://rionoticias.com.br/game-online-gratuito-ensina-educacao-financeira-a-jovens/)). Estrela's Jogo da Vida is a board game in which "most money wins". Banks run branded campaigns inside Roblox and Fortnite ([Estado de Minas](https://www.em.com.br/colunistas/educando-seu-bolso/2025/08/7234349-educacao-financeira-bancos-apostam-em-jogos-para-ensinar-jovens-a-lidar-com-dinheiro.html)). Abroad, NGPF's free browser Arcade is the closest model. STAX compresses "20 years of investing in 20 minutes", Spent simulates living paycheck to paycheck, and in Shady Sam the player is the loan shark. Each ships with a reflection worksheet ([NGPF](https://dev5.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2025-most-played-arcade-games)). NGPF's games are probably proprietary, though, so their design can be borrowed but not their code.

Against that field, the proposed game adds six things: a full life rather than a single scenario; the real 2024 cap and fatura layout; parcelamento arithmetic against à-vista discounts; entry points that match real Brazilian milestones (mesada, Aprendiz and Pé-de-Meia, first CLT job and card); a counterfactual ghost; and a clear open licence. That last point is a real differentiator, because **70.8% of sampled MEC RED resources carry no licence information** ([SBC/CBIE](https://sol.sbc.org.br/index.php/cbie_estendido/article/view/31783)). Being neutral and ad-free also lets schools trust it in a way they cannot trust a bank's Roblox campaign. Some caveats: the notes did not check whether the Brazilian titles above still work in 2026, and they did not research CVM, Anbima, Caixa, Nubank or itch.io offerings. So "nobody does this" means "nobody found", not "proven absent".

## A v1 that honours the interview: every month is a turn, not every turn is a stop

### Pacing: about 60 stops spread across whatever life remains

The owner's decisions contain one tension that has to be designed around. A life from 10 to 30 is **240 monthly turns**, and to 40 it is 360. A 15-minute run leaves 2.5–3.75 seconds per month. So every month should still *happen*: pay arrives, bills and parcelas are paid, the calendar ticks with a short animation of about 0.4 seconds. But the player sets **standing orders** ("guardar R$ X por mês na caixinha", "pagar a fatura inteira / o mínimo", "aceitar bico aos sábados"), and the game **stops** only when a choice is new. That means an event, an offer, a fatura the player cannot cover, a birthday milestone, or a periodic check-in.

The recommendation is a fixed budget of **about 60 stops of 10–12 seconds each**, plus about 2 minutes of ticking, for roughly 14–15 minutes whatever the starting age. A 10-start spends its early stops sparsely, about one check-in a quarter between 10 and 13. An 18-start gets denser stops. Every run then lasts about the same time, while an earlier start still means more months of compounding.

Score **net worth at 30 in reais of today** as the headline, because 30 is reachable by every entry point in 15 minutes. Then play a one-minute, choice-free **"e aos 40?" coda** in yearly ticks with predict-then-reveal. It projects the player's habits forward and puts both numbers on the share card, which honours the owner's "30 or 40" without lengthening play. Internally the simulation runs nominal. IPCA is drawn every January, wages follow the minimum-wage formula, and the final score is deflated so that runs and entry ages can be compared.

### Three entry points into one life

| Start | Phase | Money in | Key decisions | Lessons | Stop density |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10** | 10–13 "cofrinho" | mesada R$ 50–100/month by family model: fixed / on demand / earn it / can't afford | lanche every day vs. bring from home; cofrinho vs. kid account at 100% CDI; R$ 3 diamonds; using a parent's additional card (the bill reaches the parent) | small amounts add up, saving goals, ads and unboxing, the "free diamonds" scam | low, about quarterly |
| **14** | 14–17 "primeiro trampo" | mesada; Jovem Aprendiz R$ 737–1,105.50 + 2% FGTS; Pé-de-Meia if eligible | first phone à vista vs. 12x (R$ 125 × 12); keep ≥ 80% attendance or lose the R$ 200; secured card at 16 (limit = your own caixinha); classmate's "tigrinho"; ENEM | parcelas stacking up, locked money, "your limit is your money", bets as expected loss | medium |
| **18** | 18–30 "vida adulta" | first CLT job R$ 1,621 (≈ R$ 1,402 net); bicos; career wages | first unsecured card; move out vs. stay home; SISU / ProUni / FIES / EAD / técnico / concurso; moto in 60x vs. consórcio vs. bus; consignado offers; Black Friday | rotativo and the cap, credit ladder, career ROI, emergency fund | high |

A 10-start passes through all three phases, while a 14- or 18-start begins with zero savings and a default family. "Younger phases are shorter and simpler" here means fewer stops per year, one choice per stop and no rates on screen, only reais. The R$ 50–100 mesada comes from 2023–24 surveys. The **R$ 8–15 lanche price is unsourced**, so it should be shipped as a tunable placeholder.

### The monthly turn

Each month runs in a fixed order: income (salary, mesada, Pé-de-Meia, bico); compulsory costs (food, transport, rent or family contribution, phone); parcelas and the fatura; standing orders (saving, investing); interest accrual on every balance; the event roll; and finally the stop check. Comprometimento = committed parcelas plus loan instalments ÷ net income. It appears as a permanent gauge that turns **yellow at 30%**, the national record level, and **red at 40%**, the BCB's own alert threshold for Pix credit. When a stop happens, it offers **1–3 choices**, as decided in the interview.

### Mechanic 1: the card, with the real fatura and the real cap

The game's fatura copies the legal layout. It shows the original value of each operation, the charges accrued so far, and an options box with the minimum, the next-period cost of paying only the minimum, and financing options **ranked by CET**. Teaching the real document is worth more than a stylised one.

The parameters are as follows. Rotativo is 15.3% a month and is allowed for one cycle only. After that the player must pay in full or accept a fatura parcelada at 9.4% a month, in 3x to 12x. Each operation keeps a running charge counter that **stops accruing at 100% of its original value**. Conversion does not reset the counter, a new purchase opens a new operation, and IOF is added on top at 0.38% plus 0.0082% a day. The minimum payment is an issuer-set **placeholder, flagged unverified**.

Card access follows the real path: an additional card on a parent's account before 16, a secured card at 16–17 with the limit equal to the caixinha balance, and an unsecured card at 18. The starting limit should be a low multiple of income, tunable, because no issuer publishes first limits.

When the cap binds or a fatura goes unpaid, the consequences play out as events. The card is blocked, the player's name is negativado, the Serasa-style score falls into "Baixo", credit offers turn worse, portability offers appear, and a Desenrola-style deal may arrive. The record clears **5 years after the due date** of game time. The predict-then-reveal step asks, "Se você só pagar o mínimo, quanto vai dever daqui a 5 meses?", then shows R$ 1,000 → R$ 2,000 and how close the guess was.

### Mechanic 2: parcelamento, à vista vs. 12x, and the parcela pile-up

Every shop offer shows the à-vista or Pix price, the "12x sem juros" price, and the money the player has invested. One computed rule settles most cases. With money earning 100% of CDI net of 22.5% IR (about 0.83% a month), **12x sem juros beats paying à vista unless the Pix discount exceeds about 5.2%. For 6x the threshold is about 2.8%, and for 3x about 1.6%.** Below the threshold, "sem juros" is a free loan worth taking, *as long as each parcela fits next month's budget*. Above it, pay à vista. A teen with no savings has no choice at all, which is itself the lesson.

The real trap is stacking. Every PSJ purchase adds a slice to the next N faturas, so the comprometimento gauge climbs, and a later shock pushes the player into the minimum payment and rotativo. The offer catalogue: phones at R$ 850 / 1,300 / 1,700; the CG 160 at R$ 17,350 à vista, about R$ 550 a month in 60x (total > R$ 33,000), about R$ 732 a month in 48x after R$ 5,000 down at 1.49% a month, or a consórcio at about R$ 320 a month with delivery by draw; and a November Black Friday stop with stacked offers. The exact Pix-discount percentages are **P0 to verify** before shipping.

### Mechanic 3: investing, poupança vs. Tesouro Selic vs. CDB, with guess-then-reveal

| Instrument | Rule in game | 2026 start value | Friction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poupança | 0.5% a.m. + TR if Selic > 8.5%; else 70% of Selic + TR | ≈ 0.67% a.m. (8.3% in 12 months) | tax-free; interest only on the monthly aniversário |
| Tesouro Selic | tracks Selic | 13.75% a.a. ≈ 1.08% a.m. | 0.20% a.a. custody above R$ 10k; regressive IR; minimum ticket unverified |
| CDB / caixinha | 100–120% of CDI | 13.65% a.a. ≈ 1.07% a.m. | regressive IR 22.5→15%; IOF if redeemed within 30 days; FGC R$ 250k (some cofrinhos are not covered) |

Rates follow a seeded yearly path. The run starts at Selic 13.75%, then takes a bounded random walk between 9% and 15%, with IPCA between 3% and 6%. Poupança therefore sometimes switches to its 70%-of-Selic rule. The guess-then-reveal step fires at the first deposit, at every fifth birthday and at the coda. The reveal shows the curve with simple interest drawn beside it, so the gap opens visibly over years.

A computed calibration table for the designers: **R$ 100 a month at 5% real grows to about R$ 40,600 by age 30 if started at 10 (R$ 24,000 deposited), R$ 29,000 if started at 14, and R$ 19,500 if started at 18**. By 40 the figures are about R$ 81,500, R$ 62,700 and R$ 47,300. The lesson the owner wants, that starting earlier gives compound interest more time, therefore appears directly in the numbers.

Every investment card also carries a quick quiz on the "×12 trap": 15% a month is 435% a year, not 180%.

### Mechanic 4: career and education, spending money and months now for a higher salary later

Each month carries a **time budget of two slots**. Work, study, bicos and rest each occupy slots. Courses cost tuition and slots for a set number of months, then unlock jobs from the ladder table, subject to hiring odds by qualification: about **9% unemployment risk without ensino médio, 5.6% with a degree in progress, 3% with a degree**.

The paths are these. Finishing ensino médio (with Pé-de-Meia's locked R$ 1,000 a year as the reward) unlocks better entry jobs. A técnico (two years; SENAI/ETEC/IF cost is a **placeholder**) leads to técnico de enfermagem at R$ 3,325. ENEM → SISU (free), ProUni (by family income) or FIES (zero interest, repaid at no more than 10% of income) leads to a degree. A private EAD degree costs R$ 214 a month and a presencial one R$ 835. A concurso needs study months and has an unknown pass rate, so treat it as a long shot with stability. Dropout risk starts near the measured 58–63% and rises with debt stress and with missing rest slots, which ties careers to the credit mechanics. At 18, male characters face an alistamento roll with a small chance of 12 months at R$ 1,177, pausing other jobs and study.

Ghost attribution will show the payoff, for example "sua faculdade EAD custou R$ 10.3 mil e somou R$ X ao seu patrimônio aos 30".

### The ghost: same luck, different choices

For the ghost to be fair, **luck must not depend on choices**. Every random draw (events, rates, job offers) should come from a stream seeded by run seed, month and event type. Conditions are applied after the draw: a theft roll exists every month, but it only bites if the player owns a phone. Then two ghosts are possible. The first is "você, da última vez": the player's previous run on the same seed, which is the beat-your-own-ghost loop the owner chose. The second is "você, se...": the same life replayed with one disciplined policy, such as saving 10% of every income, paying the fatura in full and paying à vista when the discount clears the threshold.

Because the engine is pure and runs 240–360 months in milliseconds, the end screen can also re-simulate with each major decision flipped. It then names the **three choices that cost or earned the most** in reais at 30, which is the "felt cost" the Bruhn result calls for. A share code (seed, entry age and choices, compressed into a URL fragment) lets a friend play *the same life*, which suits WhatsApp. The share card carries net worth at 30 and at 40, the ghost gap, and the player's exponential-bias score.

### Net worth stays the score, with guardrails against joyless optimisation

Net worth is cash, investments, FGTS, locked Pé-de-Meia and depreciated assets, minus all remaining debt. A pure money score rewards never buying a lanche. Jogo da Vida's "most money wins" is the anti-pattern, and the notes recommend against it. Keep net worth as the only score, but make austerity cost something in the game. A small "rolê/bem-estar" need, if neglected for long, raises dropout risk and triggers a "burnout" event. A few unscored badges, such as "nunca entrou no rotativo" or "nome limpo a vida toda", celebrate other good outcomes.

### Tone: the narrator mocks the offer, not the player

The owner accepts the risk of slang dating fast, so manage it rather than avoid it. **Every narrator line lives in content files** so the community can refresh slang without touching code, and each line is tagged by phase. The 10–13 register is lighter, playful rather than biting. Point the sarcasm upward, at the "sem juros" banner, the "gerente" pushing a consignado, the scammer and the influencer's unboxing. Never point it at poverty, region, accent, family structure or the player's bad luck.

Show the cost of a bad choice in reais with a joke attached ("parabéns, você financiou um celular e uma pizza pro banco"). Don't moralise, and never let debt look cool. Banks and fintechs should be fictional and not confusable with real brands, and real influencers should not appear. Avoid dark patterns completely: no streaks, timers or loot boxes, and no reward loop around the bets event.

## Build it as a static PWA, keep its data files traceable, and collect nothing

### Stack: DOM and SVG, not a game engine

Brazil's players are on phones. Android has **about 78% of mobile share and Chrome about 78% of mobile browsing**, with Safari around 17% ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/brazil); [StatCounter browsers](https://gs.statcounter.com/browser-market-share/mobile/brazil)). 96% of 9–17-year-old internet users go online by phone, and only **37% use the internet at school** ([Cetic.br](https://www.cetic.br/media/analises/tic_kids_online_brasil_2025_principais_resultados.pdf)). A turn-based life sim is mostly cards, forms and charts, so the recommendation is **Vite + TypeScript + Svelte 5** (Preact as the alternative). Small charts can be hand-written in SVG or done with uPlot. The app should be an offline-capable PWA (vite-plugin-pwa/Workbox) that saves to IndexedDB via idb-keyval, with a localStorage fallback and `navigator.storage.persist()`.

Avoid Godot's web export. Its multi-MB payload is a poor fit for phones on mobile data, and its own docs warn about WebGL 2.0 on Safari ([Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/export/exporting_for_web.html)). Keep the economy engine as pure, seeded TypeScript functions. Use integer centavos to avoid rounding drift, and give each event family its own RNG stream (the ghost depends on it).

The tooling list: Vitest for the engine, Playwright with mobile emulation, Lighthouse CI and axe-core for performance and accessibility, and size-limit to hold a **150–200 KB gzipped** first-interactive budget (under 500 KB total first load). For i18n, pt-BR is the source locale, and `Intl.NumberFormat('pt-BR', {currency:'BRL'})` formats money. iOS Safari can evict storage after about seven days of non-use for sites not installed to the Home Screen. So ship an export code for saves, and reuse it as the share code.

### Data files that carry their own provenance

The interview fixed per-country data files. Go one step further: every parameter in `data/br/2026-11.json` should carry `value`, `unit`, `asOf`, `source` (URL) and `status` (`verified`, `secondary`, `memory`, `placeholder`). The CI then fails a release if any parameter the shipped build uses is still `memory` or `placeholder`. That turns the verification list at the end of this report into an enforced checklist rather than a document, and it makes yearly updates (new minimum wage, new Selic) a pull request against data, not code.

### Hosting: Cloudflare Pages, with no analytics or only cookieless ones

**Cloudflare Pages' free tier has unlimited static bandwidth and requests**, 500 builds a month and custom domains with TLS ([Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/)). GitHub Pages is the simpler alternative, with a soft limit of 100 GB a month ([GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)). Avoid Vercel Hobby, which is restricted to non-commercial use, in case donations or sponsorship ever arrive ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines)). Ship with **no analytics**, or at most Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and aggregate ([Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)). Self-host the fonts, because hot-linking Google Fonts sends visitor IPs to Google. Use CC0 asset packs (Kenney) and OFL fonts, and record every third-party asset's licence. For distribution through social media, the end-of-run card can be a client-rendered PNG passed to the Web Share API, which puts sharing in the player's hands without any social SDK on the page.

### Licences and repository: a GitHub organisation from day one

The owner chose MIT/Apache for code and CC BY for content. The concrete recommendation is **MIT for `/src`** (simplest and most familiar; Apache-2.0 if an explicit patent grant matters) and **CC BY 4.0 for `/content`, `/locales` and original art**. CC BY 4.0 has an official Portuguese version ([Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/list.pt)). Avoid NC terms, which complicate reuse by schools, governments and publishers. Portaria MEC 451/2018 already requires MEC-funded digital resources to be open educational resources ([Signo/UNISC](https://seer.unisc.br/index.php/signo/article/view/18891/11325)). Use a **DCO rather than a CLA**, which is lighter for teen and volunteer contributors, and keep translations inbound=outbound.

Start in a **free GitHub organisation**, not a personal account. That gives more than one owner, teams for translators, a neutral name for schools, and an easy later transfer to a Brazilian association. The suggested layout:

- `/src/engine` (pure TS, seeded, unit-tested)
- `/src/ui`
- `/content/pt-BR` (events, narrator lines, glossary)
- `/data/br`
- `/locales`
- `/assets` with `credits.json`
- `/docs` (design, pedagogy, `PRIVACY.md` in pt-BR)
- `.github/workflows` (`ci.yml`, `deploy.yml`)
- `README.md` in pt-BR first, with `CONTRIBUTING`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT`, `SECURITY`, `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT` and `ASSETS.md`

Ship a zip of the static build with each release so that a school lab can run it from a USB stick when v2's classroom mode arrives.

## The legal strategy for an audience that starts at age 10 is to collect nothing

The **ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025) has been in force since 17 March 2026**. It covers digital products directed at minors or likely to be accessed by them, and a finance game for 10–18-year-olds is plainly *directed at* them ([Machado Meyer](https://www.machadomeyer.com.br/pt/inteligencia-juridica/publicacoes-ij/direito-digital/estatuto-digital-da-crianca-e-do-adolescente-lei-n-15-211-2025-entra-em-vigor-em-17-de-marco-de-2026)). It bans profiling for advertising to children and adolescents ([Demarest](https://www.demarest.com.br/?p=164008)). It bans **loot boxes in games directed at minors** in art. 20, targeting the feature rather than the game ([Assis e Mendes](https://assisemendes.com.br/loot-boxes-eca-digital-e-monetizacao-em-jogos-2836273/)). It requires reliable age verification only for content improper for under-18s. Reported sanctions reach 10% of Brazilian revenue, up to R$ 50 million per infraction, though only one source gives that figure ([ComputerWeekly BR](https://www.computerweekly.com/br/reportagen/ECA-Digital-obrigacoes-penalidades-e-o-que-muda-para-plataformas-digitais-no-Brasil)).

LGPD art. 14 requires processing of minors' data to serve their best interest. Because the 10-start explicitly targets **children under 12, any personal data collected about them would need specific parental consent** ([LGPD](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)). The ANPD's Enunciado 1/2023 allows other legal bases, provided the best interest prevails ([Lefosse](https://lefosse.com/noticias/alerta/anpd-publica-enunciado-sobre-o-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-de-criancas-e-adolescentes/)).

The conclusion is architectural. A client-only game with **no accounts, no server-side data, no third-party trackers, pixels or ads, no purchases, no loot boxes and no chat** leaves these laws almost nothing to apply to. What remains is the duty of care, which no architecture removes. Saves stay on the device. Share codes live in the URL fragment, which the browser never sends to the server. Host logs still contain IP addresses, which are personal data, so the pt-BR privacy page should say what the game does *not* collect and name the host.

Betting law points the same way. Lei 14.790/2023 and Portaria SPA/MF 1.231/2024 treat betting advertising aimed at minors as abusive ([Lefosse](https://lefosse.com/noticias/publicidade-de-apostas-de-quota-fixa-novas-regras-ampliam-deveres-de-operadores-e-agentes-da-cadeia-de-veiculacao/)). A critical *depiction* of bets is not advertising. A *playable* random-reward minigame, though, even with fake currency, could be read as a loot box, so keep the bets event as a narrated expected loss and get legal review before adding any randomised reward.

Two more items are lower risk. The Marco Civil's six-month log retention applies to legal entities acting for profit ([Marco Civil](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2011-2014/2014/lei/l12965.htm)), so a non-commercial individual project is probably outside it. For accessibility, the Lei Brasileira de Inclusão and the government's eMAG point to WCAG ([eMAG](https://www.gov.br/governodigital/pt-br/acessibilidade-e-usuario/acessibilidade-digital/modelo-de-acessibilidade)), so target **WCAG 2.2 AA** and never signal debt by red or green alone. COPPA exposure is negligible for a no-collection pt-BR site ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)).

The limits of this analysis: the law texts could not be read verbatim (Planalto was blocked), so article numbers come from legal commentary. Decreto 12.880/2026 and the ANPD's 20 March 2026 guidance were not read, and ClassInd rating matters only if the game ever ships to an app store.

## Conclusion

The research changes what this game is for. The cap on card interest means the honest Brazilian debt story is no longer "interest grows forever". It is that a R$ 1,000 bill becomes R$ 2,000 in about five months, a 12-instalment rescue lands almost exactly at that ceiling, and the penalty then moves from arithmetic to consequences: a blocked card, a negativação that lasts five years, worse offers. A game teaching the uncapped story would be wrong, and one teaching only the arithmetic risks Bruhn's confident borrowers. On the investing side, 2026's 8% real rates make the compound-interest lesson almost too easy. Fixing those rates for a whole run would mis-teach in the opposite direction, which is why rates must drift.

The design implication is that the ghost replay is more than a retention feature. Shared luck, counterfactual re-simulation and a "three choices that cost you most" verdict are the most direct way the evidence offers to make a cost felt rather than explained, and to repeat that feeling close to real decisions. Two cheap engineering choices back the pedagogy. Parameters that carry their own provenance keep the numbers honest year after year, and collecting nothing keeps the legal exposure close to zero for an audience that starts at age ten. The next concrete steps are to verify the seven P0 numbers, register corredagrana.com and .app, open a GitHub organisation, and build the engine and the fatura screen first. That screen is where the game's central lesson lives.
