# Saving, investing and compound interest for a beginner in Brazil (as of Nov 2026), for a teen finance game

Research date: 2026-11-07. All rates are **annual (a.a.)** unless marked **a.m.** (monthly). "Nominal" = before inflation; "real" = after IPCA inflation. Figures labelled *(computed)* are my arithmetic from the cited inputs, not a source's own number.

## Benchmark rates: Selic, CDI, IPCA, inflation target, and the hyperinflation history

### Takeaway
As of November 2026 the Selic is **13.75% a.a.** (held on 4 Nov 2026). It fell from a 15% peak (reached mid-2025) through five 0.25-pt cuts between March and September 2026. CDI runs about 0.10 pt below Selic, at roughly 13.65%. IPCA was **4.26% in 2025** and the 2026 market forecast is about 5.2%. That leaves Brazil with one of the world's highest real interest rates, about **8–9% a.a.**, which makes plain fixed income unusually strong in a 2026 simulation. The inflation target has been **3% ±1.5 pt, continuous, since Jan 2025**. In 1993 inflation was **2,477%**, which is the grandparents' story.

**IPCA**

**Inflation target**
- **Continuous target, 3% centre, ±1.5 pt (band 1.5%–4.5%), in force from 1 Jan 2025.** The target counts as missed only if 12-month IPCA stays outside the band for **six consecutive months**, and the BCB governor must then explain in an open letter. Set by Decree 12.079 (June 2024) and the CMN, and valid until the CMN changes it. — [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/cmn-confirma-centro-da-meta-continua-de-inflacao-a-partir-de-2025/); [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/meta-continua-de-inflacao-lula-edita-decreto-e-preve-explicacao-do-bc-em-caso-de-descumprimento-por-6-meses-gll/); [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/guias/meta-de-inflacao/) (InvestNews gives the CMN resolution number inconsistently, so check it before quoting.)

- Poder360 headline: inflation fell "from 5,000% to two digits" with the Plano Real (5,000% refers to the 12-month peak in mid-1994). Monthly inflation peaked in June 1994, and the real began circulating on **1 Jul 1994**. — [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/inflacao-do-brasil-caiu-de-5-000-para-2-digitos-com-plano-real/)
- **URV** (Unidade Real de Valor) created **28 Feb 1994** as the first phase. Planning began in 1993 with the Programa de Ação Imediata. — [Correio Braziliense, 30/06/2024](https://edicao.correiobraziliense.com.br/correiobraziliense/2024/06/30/11338d/pdf/A07-ECO-3006-DIG.pdf); [JB](https://www.jb.com.br/economia/noticias/2009/06/29/proximo-dos-15-anos-plano-real-acabou-com-a-hiperinflacao.html)
- IPCA **1994: 916.43%**; **1995: 22.41%**. — [O Povo](https://mais.opovo.com.br/jornal/economia/2024/06/30/plano-real-completa-30-anos-entre-exitos-e-desafios.html); [Adriano Freire blog](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/inflacao-brasil-historico-30-anos-investidor)
- Cumulative inflation from Jul 1994 to Jun 2024 was **708.01%** per IBGE (NSC Total). InfoMoney gives 656% for a slightly different window. — [NSC Total](https://www.nsctotal.com.br/?p=7207462); [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2503949)

### Inferences
- *(computed)* 2,477% a year is about **31% per month**. Prices roughly doubled every ~2.6 months, so a wage held for a month lost close to a quarter of its purchasing power. 1994's 916% is about 21% a.m. A game event could show a "price of bread" counter ticking up daily.
- *(computed)* Real ex-ante Selic in Nov 2026 is about (1.1375/1.0522)−1 ≈ **8.1% a.a.** using the 2026 Focus IPCA, or ≈9.1% against 2025's 4.26%. Very few countries pay this, and it explains why a CDB at 100% CDI looks so good in 2026.
- *(computed)* Monthly equivalents: Selic 13.75% a.a. ≈ **1.08% a.m.**; CDI 13.65% ≈ 1.07% a.m.; IPCA 4.26% ≈ 0.35% a.m.
- For game realism, the 2024–2026 path gives a natural "rates go up, rates go down" cycle: 10.50 → 15.00 → 13.75 within roughly 30 months.

- Annual IPCA for 2024 (≈4.8%, from memory) was not verified in this session. October 2026 12-month IPCA was not found.

## Instruments a teen could actually use

### Takeaway
A teen's realistic menu in 2026: poupança (tax-free, about 6.17% a.a. + TR while Selic > 8.5%), fintech "caixinhas/cofrinhos" (100% CDI or more, mostly FGC-backed CDB/RDB), Tesouro Selic, Prefixado, IPCA+ and Educa+ (custody 0.20% a.a., free on Tesouro Selic up to R$10k), CDB/LCI/LCA, and B3 stocks/FIIs/ETFs. Anyone under 18 needs a CPF and a legal guardian operating the account. Poupança is losing money to other products (net outflow of R$85.6 bn in 2025) but still holds about R$1 trillion.

### Cited Findings
**Poupança**
- Rule (Lei 12.703/2012, from MP 567/2012): **Selic > 8.5% a.a. ⇒ 0.5% a.m. + TR; Selic ≤ 8.5% ⇒ 70% of Selic + TR.** It applies to deposits from 4 May 2012. Deposits up to 3 May 2012 ("poupança antiga") earn 0.5% a.m. + TR regardless of Selic. Interest is credited on each deposit's monthly **"aniversário"** (the deposit's date). — [Clube dos Poupadores](https://clubedospoupadores.com/poupanca/rende-meio-por-cento.html); [Contábeis, MP 567/2012](https://www.contabeis.com.br/legislacao/161952/medida-provisoria-567-2012/); [Exame](https://exame.com/invest/minhas-financas/poupanca-volta-a-rentabilidade-antiga-com-alta-da-selic)
- 2025: net outflow of **R$85.568 bn** (5th consecutive year of net withdrawals). Deposits were R$4.272 tn and withdrawals R$4.358 tn. Interest credited was R$75.858 bn. **Year-end balance: R$1.022 trillion.** December 2025 was positive (+R$5.41 bn). — [Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/economia-br/brasileiros-sacaram-r-856-bilhoes-da-poupanca-em-2025); [Economic News Brasil](https://economicnewsbrasil.com.br/2026/01/09/saque-da-poupanca-2025/)
- End-2024 balance: R$1.03 trillion, the smallest outflow since 2020. — [Abrainc](https://www.abrainc.org.br/credito-imobiliario/2025/01/09/poupanca-fecha-2024-com-menor-saida-de-recursos-desde-2020-e-saldo-final-de-r-103-trilhao)
- Pé-de-Meia payments go into a **Caixa Tem poupança account** opened automatically in the student's name. Poupança is therefore the first account for millions of low-income teens (see the Pé-de-Meia section). — [SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)

**Tesouro Direto**
- Custody fee (B3): **0.20% a.a.** on the amount invested (cut from 0.25% on 1 Jan 2022). **Tesouro Selic is exempt up to R$10,000 per CPF**, with the fee charged only on the excess. — [Tesouro Direto, "Entenda a nova taxa de custódia"](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/en/b/entenda-a-nova-taxa-de-custodia); [B3 tariff page](https://b3.com.br/pt_br/produtos-e-servicos/tarifas/tarifas-de-tesouro-direto/)
- Timing of the fee charge: the semiannual charge was reportedly ended from 31 Dec 2024. The fee is now collected at maturity, on early redemption, or on coupon payments (secondary sources; confirm with B3). — [Investidor10](https://investidor10.com.br/noticias/b3-b3sa3-poe-fim-em-cobranca-semestral-de-taxa-do-tesouro-direto-entenda-109182/); [A Revista](https://arevista.com.br/renda-fixa/tesouro-direto-2026-novos-titulos-operacoes-24h-e-mudancas-de-taxas-que-vao-impactar-todos-os-investidores/)
- Yields on **16 Oct 2026**: **Prefixado 2029 at 12.79% a.a.** (up from 12.64%); Prefixado 2032 and Prefixado com Juros Semestrais 2037 at 12.95%; **Educa+ 2044 at IPCA + 6.75%**. The move followed an election-poll (Datafolha) story. — [InvestNews, 16/10/2026](https://investnews.com.br/investimentos/tesouro-direto-taxas-rendimento-hoje-prefixados-datafolha-16102026/amp/)
- Minors can hold Tesouro in their own CPF. Parents open the account in the minor's name through "Cadastro Rápido" with gov.br login, and **any title can be bought on a minor's CPF, with no age restriction**. Tesouro Direto runs a "TD Família" programme. — [Tesouro Direto, TD Família](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/b/td-familia-ensine-seus-filhos-a-investir); [Eu Quero Investir](https://euqueroinvestir.com/educacao-financeira/investimentos-para-menores-criancas)
- B3 runs a 2026 incentive programme for brokers to expand the Tesouro Direto investor base (growth in active investors, net inflows, retention, reinvestment of 2026 maturities), plus a programme for a 24x7 platform. — [B3 Ofício Circular 013/2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/6C/D0/28/30/AEF4D9101073F4D9AC094EA8/OC%20013-2026%20PRE%20PROGRAMA%20DE%20INCENTIVO%20DA%20EXPANSAO%20DA%20BASE%20DE%20INVESTIDORES%20DO%20TESOURO%20DIRETO%202026_PT.pdf); [B3 OC 034/2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/D1/34/6D/30/D492F91005A262F9AC094EA8/OC%20034-2026-VPC_PROGRAMA%20DE%20INCENTIVO%20PARA%20A%20INTEGRACAO%20A%20PLATAFORMA%2024X7%20DO%20PROGRAMA%20DO%20TESOURO%20DIRETO%202026_PT.pdf)

### Inferences
- Game design: poupança ≈ 0.5% a.m. (+TR ≈ 0) versus caixinha 100% CDI ≈ 1.07% a.m. gross in 2026. The caixinha pays roughly twice the monthly interest even after IR (see below). This is the cleanest "same app, different box" lesson.
- A "too good to be true" card fits the data well. Braiscompany-style fixed 5–10% a.m. promises against a real risk-free rate of ~0.65% a.m. make the gap obvious.
- FGC is per CPF **per institution** (R$250k). Products that are not FGC-covered (some cofrinhos backed by funds) make a good "read the fine print" mechanic.

## Taxation (regressive IR, IOF, exemptions, 2025–2026 reforms)

### Takeaway
Fixed income (CDB, Tesouro, caixinhas) pays IR on gains of **22.5% → 20% → 17.5% → 15%** by holding period. IOF eats gains on redemptions within 30 days. **Poupança, LCI/LCA and FII distributions are IR-exempt for individuals.** Stock sales up to **R$20k/month** are exempt. The 2025 attempt to tax LCI/LCA (MP 1303) **lapsed on 8 Oct 2025**, and the Lei 15.270/2025 income-tax reform (salary exemption up to R$5k/month, minimum tax on high incomes, 10% withholding on dividends above R$50k/month) took effect in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- IR regressive table (fixed income), applied to gains only: **22.5% up to 180 days; 20% 181–360; 17.5% 361–720; 15% above 720 days**. — [Safra O Especialista](https://oespecialista.safra.com.br/tabela-regressiva-ir-iof-renda-fixa/); [PagBank blog](https://blog.pagbank.com.br/tabela-ir-e-iof-em-investimentos-tudo-que-voce-precisa-saber)
- **IOF regressive**: 96% of the gain on day 1, falling to 0% from day 30. IOF is charged first, then IR applies to the remainder. — [Adriano Freire, IOF](https://www.adrianofreire.com.br/blog/iof-investimentos-prazos-30-dias-evitar); [Valor Final IOF table](https://valorfinal.com.br/tabela-iof-renda-fixa)
- **Stock exemption**: net gains on spot-market stock sales are IR-exempt when **total sales in the month are ≤ R$20,000**. The test is the amount sold, not the profit. Above that, gains are taxed at **15% (swing trade)** and **20% (day trade)**. **ETFs do not get the R$20k exemption.** FII unit sales are taxed at 20% on the gain with no R$20k exemption (this is how I read one source's "20% sem isenção" line, which refers to fund units). — [Safra](https://oespecialista.safra.com.br/tabela-regressiva-ir/); [Valor Final guide](https://valorfinal.com.br/guia/imposto-de-renda-sobre-investimentos); [Nubank blog](https://blog.nubank.com.br/imposto-de-renda-investimentos/)
- **MP 1303/2025** (the alternative to the IOF decree) proposed ending LCI/LCA exemption at 5%. The rapporteur Carlos Zarattini raised that to 7.5%, then backed down and restored the exemption. The Câmara took it off the agenda and **the MP lost validity on 8 Oct 2025** (reported 9 Oct 2025). LCI/LCA exemption therefore stands. — [InfoMoney (7.5%)](https://www.infomoney.com.br/mercados/relator-da-mp-alternativa-ao-iof-propoe-aumento-de-aliquota-para-lci-e-lca-em-75/); [IBET (recuo)](https://www.ibet.com.br/relator-recua-para-viabilizar-mp-e-decide-isentar-lcas-e-lcis/); [Rota da Jurisprudência](https://rotadajurisprudencia.com.br/tag/lci/); [InfoMoney (vote postponed to eve of expiry)](https://www.infomoney.com.br/politica/congresso-adia-votacao-de-mp-alternativa-ao-iof-para-vespera-do-prazo-de-validade/)
- **Lei 15.270/2025** (published Nov 2025, effective 2026): IR is **zero on monthly income up to R$5,000** (R$60k/year), with a partial reduction up to R$7,350/month (R$88.2k/year), done through a reduction mechanism and not a new bracket. **10% withholding** applies on dividends above R$50k/month from one company to one person. A minimum IRPF (IRPFM) for annual income above R$600k rises linearly to **10% above R$1.2 m**. The first full effect shows in the 2027 declaration. — [Mattos Filho](https://www.mattosfilho.com.br/unico/lei-tributacao-dividendos/); [PwC Tax Intelligence ed. 48](https://www.pwc.com.br/pt/thinking-about-taxes/tax-intelligence/2025/tax-intelligence-ed-48-tributacao-de-dividendos.pdf); [Viva](https://viva.com.br/dinheiro/isencao-de-imposto-de-renda-para-quem-ganha-ate-r-5-mil-vira-lei.html); [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/financas/novo-ir-sobre-dividendos-2027/)

### Inferences
- *(computed)* A CDB/caixinha at 100% CDI (13.65%) nets **10.58% a.a. if redeemed within 180 days** (22.5% IR) and **11.60% if held more than 2 years** (15% IR). Poupança's gross ≈6.17% + TR stays untaxed. In 2026 the after-tax caixinha still beats poupança by ~4.4–5.4 pts a year. The tax break only favours poupança when Selic is low and the 70% rule applies.
- For an under-18 player the R$5k exemption is mostly irrelevant, but it suits a "first job" event card. A teen earning a salary of R$2,000 pays no IR.
- LCI/LCA "tax-free 90–95% of CDI" versus a "taxable 100–110% of CDI CDB" makes a good gross-up puzzle: 0.9 ÷ 0.85 ≈ 106% equivalent at the 15% rate.

### Gaps
- No Receita Federal primary page fetched. The IR/IOF tables come from bank and broker educational sites, which agree with one another.
- LCI/LCA minimum lock-up after the CMN Feb 2024 resolution was not verified this session.
- I did not check whether any post-Oct-2025 bill revived LCI/LCA taxation in 2026.

## Retirement and the long horizon (INSS, FGTS, PGBL/VGBL, start-at-18 vs 30)

### Takeaway
After the 2019 reform, the permanent INSS rule is retirement at **62 (women) / 65 (men)** with a minimum contribution period (15 years for women; 20 years for men who entered after the reform, the latter only from memory). The 2026 ceiling is **R$8,475.55** and the floor is the minimum wage, **R$1,621**. FGTS earns **TR + 3%** plus profit distribution, giving **6.90% total for 2025** against IPCA of 4.26%. Compound illustrations show starting at 18 roughly doubles the real end balance of starting at 30, depending on the rate.

### Cited Findings
- INSS permanent rule (for those who began contributing after EC 103/2019): **62 for women and 65 for men**, with a minimum of 15 years' contribution for women. Transition rules in 2026: progressive minimum age of **59y6m (women) / 64y6m (men)**, plus 30/35 years' contribution. The points rule requires **93 (women) / 103 (men)** in 2026, rising to 100/105 by 2033. — [Econet](https://blog.econeteditora.com.br/aposentadoria-2026-regras-inss/); [O Povo, 03/01/2026](https://mais.opovo.com.br/jornal/economia/2026/01/03/aposentadoria-inss-regras-de-pontos-e-idade-minima-ficam-mais-rigidas-em-2026.html); [Folha PE](https://www.folhape.com.br/economia/regras-do-inss-mudam-em-2026-e-podem-alterar-valor-da-aposentadoria/488153/)
- **INSS ceiling 2026: R$8,475.55** (up from R$8,157.41). Floor = **minimum wage R$1,621.00 (2026)**. — [NSC Total](https://www.nsctotal.com.br/economia/teto-inss-2026-mudancas-aposentadoria)
- Half the minimum wage per person = **R$810.50 (2026)**, the Pé-de-Meia income line. This is consistent with R$1,621. — [SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)
- **FGTS**: legal remuneration is **TR + 3% a.a.** plus profit distribution. For 2025 the Conselho Curador distributed **R$13.04 bn (89% of R$14.65 bn profit)**, adding **1.87 pts** for a **6.90% total return in 2025**. Credited by 31 Aug 2026 on balances as of 31 Dec 2025. Under a 2024 STF ruling, if TR + 3% + distribution falls below IPCA, the Conselho Curador must make up the difference. — [CBIC](https://cbic.org.br/conselho-curador-do-fgts-aprova-distribuicao-de-r-13-bilhoes-do-lucro-de-2025/); [Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2026/07/fgts-distribuicao-lucro-2025-quanto-trabalhador-recebe/); [Movimento Econômico](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/2026/07/28/fgts-distribuira-r-13-bilhoes-do-lucro-de-2025-a-1382-milhoes-de-trabalhadores/)
- **Saque-aniversário**: an optional annual withdrawal of part of the balance in the birthday month. Workers who opt in and are later fired lose access to the full balance, with only a residual withdrawal. — [CBIC](https://cbic.org.br/conselho-curador-do-fgts-aprova-distribuicao-de-r-13-bilhoes-do-lucro-de-2025/); [Diário de Pernambuco](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/economia/2026/07/11719961-caixa-comeca-a-depositar-lucro-do-fgts-de-2025-nesta-quinta-feira-30.html)
- Youth retirement attitudes: Neofeed's headline on the Anbima Gen Z data reads "cripto em alta, previdência em baixa e boom das bets". InvestNews reports that Brazilians prefer crypto "even for retirement". — [Neofeed](https://neofeed.com.br/economia/cripto-em-alta-previdencia-em-baixa-e-boom-das-bets-a-dieta-financeira-da-geracao-z/); [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/investimentos/nem-acoes-nem-tesouro-brasileiros-preferem-criptomoedas-ate-na-aposentadoria/)

### Inferences — compound illustrations *(computed; constant real rates, monthly compounding, R$ of today)*
R$100/month, real (after-inflation) return:

| Real rate a.a. | Start 18 → 65 (R$56,400 in) | Start 30 → 65 (R$42,000 in) | Save 18–30 only, then stop (R$14,400 in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | 56,400 | 42,000 | 14,400 |
| 2% | 93,023 | 60,541 | 32,481 |
| 4% | 162,439 | 89,992 | 72,447 |
| 6% | 297,191 | 137,360 | 159,830 |

- At 6% real, saving only from 18 to 30 (R$14.4k in) ends **above** saving from 30 to 65 (R$42k in). This is the classic "early bird" result, built from Brazilian-plausible real rates: Tesouro IPCA+ has paid ~6–7.8% real at points in 2024–2026, and Educa+ 2044 was at IPCA + 6.75% on 16 Oct 2026.
- FGTS's 6.90% in 2025 versus IPCA of 4.26% is about **2.5% real**, so it earned more than inflation in 2025, unlike many TR-only years.

### Gaps
- Men's minimum contribution for post-reform entrants (20 years) was not confirmed by the sources found. Only women's 15 years was confirmed.
- PGBL/VGBL rules (PGBL deductible up to 12% of gross taxable income; VGBL taxes gains only; progressive vs regressive 35%→10% tables, and Lei 14.803/2024 letting the choice be deferred) were not researched this session. They are from memory and should be treated as unverified.
- No survey quantifying youth retirement expectations (for example, the share who expect to rely on INSS) was found.

## Pé-de-Meia (2024–): what it is, amounts, eligibility, payout

### Takeaway
Pé-de-Meia is the federal (MEC/Caixa) savings incentive for low-income **public-school high-school students aged 14–24** registered in CadÚnico. It pays **up to R$9,200 over the three years**: R$200 per year for enrolment, up to 9 × R$200 per year for attendance, and R$1,000 per completed grade held back until graduation, plus R$200 for sitting ENEM. Payment goes into an automatically opened **Caixa Tem poupança**. The name is also a common word for "nest egg", so a game named "Pé-de-Meia" would collide directly with the programme.

### Cited Findings
- Amounts (regular high school): incentivo-matrícula **R$200/year**; incentivo-frequência **up to 9 × R$200/year (R$1,800)**; incentivo-conclusão **R$1,000 per completed grade, withdrawable only after graduating**; **+R$200 for taking ENEM in 3rd year**. **Total up to R$9,200 over three years.** EJA: R$225 attendance instalments, up to 4 per semester. — [SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/); [Trademap, Aug 2026](https://trademap.com.br/noticias/news-8218339)
- Eligibility: ages **14–24**, enrolled in public high school, family in **CadÚnico** with income **≤ ½ minimum wage per person (R$810.50 in 2026)**. CadÚnico cut-off date for the 2026 cycle was **7 Aug 2026**. Attendance must be **≥80%** or that month's instalment is withheld. Enrolment is automatic for those meeting the criteria. — [SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)
- Payment: into a **Caixa Tem poupança account opened automatically by Caixa**, staggered by birth month. Students can check it in the "Jornada do Estudante" app, Caixa Tem, or MEC 0800 616161. 2026 instalments were paid on a schedule through mid-2026 (3rd in May, 4th from late June, 5th in July/Aug). — [DGABC](https://www.dgabc.com.br/Noticia/4343593/pe-de-meia-paga-5-parcela-a-alunos-nascidos-em-julho-e-agosto); [Diário de Pernambuco](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/brasil/2026/05/11714921-pe-de-meia-2026-terceira-parcela-comeca-a-ser-paga-nesta-segunda-25.html); [Tribuna do Sertão](https://tribunadosertao.com.br/geral/2026/06/29/930502-pe-de-meia-2026-pagamento-da-quarta-parcela-comeca-nesta-segunda)

### Inferences
- *(computed)* If the R$3,000 of conclusion incentives (3 × R$1,000) sat in poupança at ~6.17% a.a. nominal while being held back, the effect would be modest. The real lesson is the "locked-until-graduation" rule, a built-in commitment device that maps neatly onto a game mechanic (bonus unlocked by finishing a level).
- Name collision: "pé-de-meia" is also the everyday idiom for savings. Using it as a game title risks confusion with an official MEC programme, and scammers have plausibly exploited the name. The scam angle is not verified.

### Gaps
- Official MEC or gov.br primary page not fetched. Figures come from press and aggregators that agree with one another.
- Number of beneficiaries (millions of students) and total budget for 2026 were not found this session.
- Whether instalments earn poupança interest while held, and the exact unlock and withdrawal rules for the conclusion bonus, were not confirmed.

## How compound interest is taught in Brazil, and literacy and misconceptions data

### Takeaway
Since the 2018 BNCC, financial education is a mandatory "tema contemporâneo transversal" anchored in high-school Mathematics. The BNCC asks students to compare **juros simples versus compostos** and to recognise exponential growth. Results are weak: Brazil scored **416 in PISA 2022 financial literacy** against an OECD mean of 498, with about **45% below baseline Level 2** (OECD 18%), unchanged since 2018. Youth face heavy competition for their money from bets and scams.

### Cited Findings
- BNCC: financial education became a contemporary transversal theme from 2018 and mandatory from 2020, located in the Mathematics section. Schools decide how to articulate it. BNCC high school requires distinguishing **juros simples and compostos, highlighting exponential growth**, and work with percentages, often using spreadsheets. — [UERJ e-publicações](https://www.e-publicacoes.uerj.br/riae/article/download/52245/40439/226526); [Mackenzie repository](https://dspace.mackenzie.br/handle/10899/33765); [PUC-SP](https://revistas.pucsp.br/index.php/pdemat/article/viewFile/51085/pdf)
- **PISA 2022 financial literacy, Brazil: 416 points** (OECD average 498). Brazil was among 10 systems below the OECD average. **~45% below Level 2** (OECD 18%); **~2% at Level 5** (OECD 11%). The score was similar to 2018 and about 22 points above 2015. The gender gap was not significant. The gap between advantaged and disadvantaged students was 86 points (OECD 87). — [OECD PISA 2022 Vol IV Brazil factsheet](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-iv-factsheets_34d60137-en/brazil_1c815ef9-en.html)
- PISA 2022 maths context: **73% of Brazilian students are below the basic level in mathematics**. This matters because compound interest is taught inside maths. — [Correio Braziliense, Dec 2023](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/euestudante/2023/12/6665288-pisa-73-dos-estudantes-brasileiros-estao-abaixo-do-nivel-em-matematica.html)
- **Serasa (Mar 2026, 10 years of the Mapa da Inadimplência)**: **81.7 m** Brazilians in default, +38.1% versus 2016. **332 m** debts, +43%. The share of 18–25-year-olds among defaulters fell from **15.93% to 11.45%**, while the 60+ share grew. The series hit a record **83.7 m in June 2026**. A press report gives 84.2 m in Sep 2026, with 18–25-year-olds at 11%. — [Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/10-anos-do-mapa-de-inadimplencia/); [IstoÉ](https://istoe.com.br/brasil-conta-em-2026-com-332-milhoes-de-dividas-volume-43-maior-que-em-2016-diz-serasa/); [Serasa Mapa](https://www.serasa.com.br/limpa-nome-online/blog/mapa-da-inadimplencia-e-renogociacao-de-dividas-no-brasil); [Tribuna de Jundiaí](https://tribunadejundiai.com.br/economia/inadimplencia-brasil-recorde-serasa/)
- More than 1.5 m young people used Serasa Limpa Nome in Jan–Jul 2025. Gen Z is the fastest-growing group in debt renegotiations (regional data, Tocantins). — [Conexão TO](https://conexaoto.com.br/2025/08/18/geracao-z-e-o-publico-que-mais-cresce-nas-negociacoes-de-dividas-no-tocantins)
- Gen Z's learning channels are YouTube (49%) and Instagram (45%) (Anbima 2026). Influencers, not school, are their main source. — [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=3305370)

### Inferences
- Textbook framing in Brazil uses montante M = C(1+i)^t with **monthly rates (a.m.)**. *(computed)* At 1% a.m. on R$1,000, simple versus compound gives R$1,120 vs R$1,126.83 after 12 months and **R$2,200 vs R$3,300.39 after 120 months**. A side-by-side curve that only diverges visibly after years makes a good game visual.
- Common misconception to target: treating an a.m. rate × 12 as the a.a. rate. *(computed)* 1% a.m. is 12.68% a.a., not 12%. Poupança's "0.5% a.m." is 6.17% a.a. The reverse trap is credit-card and loan rates quoted monthly.
- With 45% below PISA Level 2, many teens may not reliably compare two offers. Game mechanics should therefore teach comparison (percent-of-CDI, a.m. vs a.a., gross vs net) explicitly rather than assume it.
- The "dinheiro trabalhando para você" ("your money working for you") framing is ubiquitous in Brazilian investing content, but this session did not source a specific originator.

## Realistic return assumptions for a game (poupança vs Tesouro Selic vs CDB vs stock index, 1/5/10/30 years)

### Takeaway
For 2026-calibrated play, use these rough figures. Poupança: ~6.2% nominal, ~1.5–2% real. Tesouro Selic / CDB at 100% CDI: ~13.7% gross (~11.6% net after 2 years), ~5% real over the long run but ~7–8% real right now. Ibovespa: historically a **7.6% mean / 4.4% median real a.a. over 20-year windows since 1967**, with huge volatility (+34% in 2025). The CDI beat the Ibovespa over many recent 10–20-year windows, so stocks should not be modelled as a sure winner.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 scorecard: **Ibovespa +33.95%**, **CDI +14.32–14.33%**, IMA-B 5 +11.65%, S&P 500 (BRL) +16.4%. — [Daycoval](https://blog.daycoval.com.br/fundos-ibovespa-cdi/); [Numerando CDI 2025](https://www.numerando.com.br/indices/cdi/2025)
- 10 years to 2025 (Economatica, cited): **CDI ≈ +131% versus Ibovespa ≈ +80%**. R$100k in a CDI CDB → ~R$231k, Ibovespa tracker → ~R$180k (gross, before tax). InfoMoney reports a different 10-year Ibovespa figure (+271.7%), so the result depends on the window. — [Renova Invest](https://renovainvest.com.br/blog/cdi-x-ibovespa); [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=3415507) — **conflicting**
- A fund manager (via Money Times): CDI beat Ibovespa over the last 20 years. Since 1967 the Ibovespa delivered a **7.6% a.a. average real return over 20-year windows, median 4.4%**. — [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/ibovespa-precisaria-subir-86-a-285-mil-pontos-para-voltar-a-mediana-calcula-gestora-rnda/)
- 2003–2023: poupança returned **+233.66%** versus IPCA **+206.20%** (barely positive in real terms). In 2013–2023, poupança **lost** to IPCA. The Ibovespa beat Selic and IPCA+5% over 2003–2023 in that study. — [Clube dos Poupadores](https://clubedospoupadores.com/carteira-investimentos/longo.html)
- A May 2026 columnist notes that the chosen window almost decides the winner: CDI tends to win short horizons and IPCA+ often wins 10–15-year ones. — [Investimentos.com.br, May 2026](https://investimentos.com.br/artigos/cdi-ou-ipca-quem-leva-a-melhor/)
