# Brazilian Consumer Credit Landscape for Young People (ages 14-25), as of November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-07. Unless noted, rates are Banco Central do Brasil (BCB) averages for **pessoa física (PF)**, taken from its monthly "Estatísticas Monetárias e de Crédito" release as reported in the press. **The BCB headline figures are ANNUAL (% a.a.).** Monthly equivalents marked "(calc.)" are my own conversions using i_m = (1 + i_a)^(1/12) - 1. They are not BCB-published numbers.

Source-quality note: the 2026 figures mostly come from wire-service stories (Agência Estado/O Povo, Agência Brasil) that relay BCB, CNC and Serasa releases. I fetched the primary pages where they allowed it. Several secondary sites (wscom, DIEESE, caixa.gov.br) blocked fetching, so some figures rest on search snippets and are marked that way.

## 1. Credit cards: rotativo, parcelamento da fatura, pagamento mínimo, the 100% cap, "parcelado sem juros", youth limits, fintech culture

### Takeaway
The card revolving rate (rotativo) is about **452% a.a. (~15.3% a.m., calc.) as of September 2026**, and the fatura instalment plan (parcelado) is about **193% a.a. (~9.4% a.m., calc.)**. Since 3 Jan 2024 the law has capped the total interest plus charges on rotativo and parcelamento de fatura at **100% of the original debt**. At rotativo rates the cap is reached in roughly 5 months. About **42% of all credit-card spending in Brazil is "parcelado sem juros" (PSJ)** (Abecs, 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Rates (BCB, PF):**
- **September 2026: rotativo 451.7% a.a.; parcelado (fatura instalment) 193.0% a.a.; "custo total do cartão" 100.3% a.a.** BCB released these on 29 Oct 2026. The BCB annualises the monthly rate over 12 months. — [WSCOM, 30/10/2026](https://wscom.com.br/economia/2026/10/30/juro-do-rotativo-do-cartao-de-credito-sobe-para-4517-ao-ano-em-setembro-aponta-banco-central/) (seen as a search snippet only; the page returned HTTP 403 when fetched)
- Earlier 2026 levels: rotativo **424.5% a.a. in January 2026** (parcelado 194.9% a.a.) and **435.9% a.a. in February 2026**. — figures from search aggregation of [Mais Retorno](https://maisretorno.com/portal/tudo-sobre/rotativo) and [Digio blog](https://www.digio.com.br/blog/salvando-grana/juros-do-rjotativo-do-cartao-de-credito-seguem-em-patamar-alto-e-acendem-alerta-para-o-bolso/). **Caution:** one Mais Retorno URL in the results is titled "juro do rotativo subiu em fevereiro a 355,2% ao ano", which is probably an older year. I could not open these pages to tie each number to its month, so treat Jan/Feb 2026 as approximate. The trend in 2026 is clearly flat-to-rising in the 420-450% a.a. band.
- Rotativo was about **431.6% a.a. in 2023**, before the cap. — [Poder360 / CMN coverage, Dec 2023](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/juro-do-cartao-nao-pode-ultrapassar-valor-da-divida-decide-governo/)

**The 100% cap (Lei 14.690/2023 + Resolução CMN 5.112/2023):**
- The cap was created by the Desenrola law (Lei 14.690/2023) and regulated by CMN Resolução 5.112 (21 Dec 2023), **in force since 3 Jan 2024**. Total interest plus charges on a rotativo or parcelamento-de-fatura debt **cannot exceed the original amount**. A BCB adviser's example: on a R$100 debt the maximum collectable is **R$200 + IOF**. — [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/399499/cmn-regulamenta-lei-do-desenrola-e-limita-juros-do-rotativo-a-100); [O Povo, 22/12/2023](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-saiba-como-vai-funcionar-a-partir-de-2024.html)
- Converting rotativo into parcelamento **does not restart** the 100% counter; it runs from the start of the rotativo. The cap **does not apply to cartão consignado** (card paid by payroll deduction). — [O Povo, 22/12/2023](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-saiba-como-vai-funcionar-a-partir-de-2024.html); [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/juro-do-cartao-nao-pode-ultrapassar-valor-da-divida-decide-governo/)
- Disclosure rules: the fatura must show the original amount of each financed operation and the interest and charges accrued so far. It must also have a "payment options" area with only three items: (1) the **pagamento mínimo**, (2) the charges for the next period if only the minimum is paid, and (3) the financing options **ranked from lowest to highest CET**. — [O Povo, 22/12/2023](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-saiba-como-vai-funcionar-a-partir-de-2024.html)
- **Free portability of the fatura debt** (rotativo plus parcelamento) to another institution started on **1 Jul 2024**. If the original creditor counter-offers, it must present at least one consolidated proposal with the same term. — [Seu Dinheiro, Dec 2023](https://www.seudinheiro.com/2023/financas-pessoais/tem-dividas-no-cartao-de-credito-cmn-limita-juros-do-rotativo-a-100-e-voce-ainda-vai-poder-transferir-seus-debitos-miql); [Movimento Econômico](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-do-cartao-de-credito-limitados-100-da-divida/)

**Parcelado sem juros (PSJ) and its share of card spending (Abecs):**
- **Q1 2025:** PSJ was **41.2%** of credit-card purchase value (**R$335 bn**). **63.4%** of PSJ volume was in **up to 6 instalments**. — [InfoMoney, Q1 2025](https://www.infomoney.com.br/minhas-financas/pagamentos-com-cartoes-movimentaram-r-11-trilhao-no-primeiro-trimestre-de-2025/amp/)
- **Q3 2025:** PSJ was **42.7%** of credit-card value (**R$381.2 bn**); à vista was 57.1%. — [Abecs presentation 3T25 (PDF via Finsiders)](https://uploads.finsidersbrasil.com.br/2025/11/Abecs-Apresentacao-3T25-1.pdf)
- **2024:** PSJ was about **41%** of card value. **Full year 2025:** total card volume (credit + debit + prepaid) was **R$4.5 trillion (+10.1%)**, and "more than 42%" of credit-card volume was PSJ. — Abecs, via search summary of [Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/pela-primeira-vez-uso-de-cartoes-supera-r-4-trilhoes-em-um-ano/) and [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/financas/cartao-credito-parcelas-juros/). I did not pin the 2025 full-year figure to a single primary Abecs page.
- Brazil has passed **200 million active credit cards**. — [Monitor Mercantil (headline)](https://monitormercantil.com.br/brasil-ultrapassa-200-milhoes-de-cartoes-de-credito-ativos/) (headline only, undated in results)
- A retail association has claimed that ending PSJ would remove **about 10% of retail sales**. — [Monitor Mercantil (headline)](https://monitormercantil.com.br/fim-do-parcelado-sem-juros-tira-cerca-de-10-das-vendas-do-com-rcio-diz-associa-o/) (headline only)

**Desconto à vista vs parcelado:**
- **Lei 13.455/2017** (from MP 764/2016) lets merchants **charge different prices by payment method or term**, for example cheaper in cash or Pix than on card. A merchant that gives à vista discounts must post a visible notice. — [Dizer o Direito](https://www.dizerodireito.com.br/2017/06/lei-134552017-comercianteprestador-de.html); [Câmara, text of law](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei/2017/lei-13455-26-junho-2017-785093-norma-pl.html)
- Idec's critical view: the law lets merchants pass on costs that are really theirs (card-machine fees, and the acquirer's settlement delay on instalment sales) to the customer. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/consultas/dicas-e-direitos/preco-diferenciado-no-pagamento-com-carto-pode)

**Youth and first cards:**
- Serasa: card applications grew **111%**, and **more than one third came from people aged 20-29** (Gen Z leads). Young people's **average card spending is about R$1,250**. — [O Povo PB](https://www.opovopb.com.br/procura-por-cartao-de-credito-cresce-no-brasil-geracao-z-lidera-solicitacoes-mas-endividamento-preocupa/) (secondary; period of the 111% growth unclear)

### Inferences
- **Game mechanic, the cap in action (calc.):** at 451.7% a.a. ≈ 15.3% a.m., compound interest doubles a balance in ln2/ln1.153 ≈ **4.9 months**. A player who only pays the minimum hits the legal 100% ceiling in about 5 months (plus IOF). After that the debt stops growing from interest. In practice the bank renegotiates or the debt goes to collection and negativação. Without the cap, 12 months of rotativo would multiply the debt about 5.5 times.
- Monthly equivalents (calc.) for Sept 2026: rotativo ≈ **15.3% a.m.**; parcelado de fatura ≈ **9.4% a.m.**; "custo total do cartão" ≈ 6.0% a.m.
- "Parcelado sem juros" is rarely truly free. The merchant pays a higher MDR and an anticipation cost (or waits up to 12 months for the money), and Lei 13.455 lets it build that into the sticker price. **Rational rule for the game:** if the à vista or Pix discount is larger than what the money would earn invested over the instalment period, pay à vista. If there is no discount, PSJ is a 0% loan and taking it is rational, but only if each parcela still fits next month's budget.
- The real youth trap is not one 12x purchase. It is **stacking many PSJ purchases**, so that future faturas are pre-committed (see Section 2), which then forces a minimum payment and rotativo.

### Gaps
- **The current pagamento mínimo rule:** I did not confirm it this session. Background knowledge: since 2017, CMN Res. 4.549 requires rotativo to be used for at most **one billing cycle**, after which the balance must be moved into a cheaper instalment plan. The previous 15% regulatory minimum was later left to issuers. Confirm on bcb.gov.br (Meu BC: "cartão de crédito") before using it in the game.
- **Minimum age for a credit card:** not sourced. Commonly 18 for a titular card. Dependent or additional cards are sometimes available from 16 (or for teens via "conta teen" products at Nubank, Inter and others). Needs confirmation from issuer pages.
- **Typical starting limits** for first-time or young cardholders: no source found. Fintechs often start limits at very low values (R$50-R$500) and raise them with use, but that is unverified.
- I found no specific 2025-26 data on the market shares of Nubank, Inter, PicPay and Mercado Pago or on "limite" culture.
- **MDR levels** for credit à vista vs parcelado: not found.

## 2. Parcelamento culture, BNPL / Pix parcelado, crediário, comprometimento da renda

### Takeaway
Instalments run through Brazilian consumption: about 42% of card spending is PSJ, and the share is stable or rising. Household **income committed to debt service reached about 29% in Aug 2026, a record**. The "Pix parcelado" brand was **banned by BCB in Dec 2025**. Credit offered at Pix checkout will be regulated from **1 Jul 2027** (prior opt-in, 24h wait, alerts at 40% of payment capacity, and a new "Painel de Dívidas Consolidadas").

### Cited Findings
- **BCB household-debt indicators (August 2026):** household debt to the financial system was **50.1% of annual income** (series record; 30.9→31.0% excluding mortgages). **Comprometimento da renda (monthly income going to debt service): 28.8% (Jul) → 29.0% (Aug 2026)**; excluding mortgages, 26.5% → 26.7%. — [O Povo/Estadão, 29/10/2026](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2026/10/29/juro-medio-no-credito-livre-sobe-a-490-em-setembro-mostra-bc-cheque-especial-vai-a-139.html)
- FGV IBRE describes income commitment at the highest level in the BCB series, with credit expansion becoming "more expensive". — [Blog do IBRE/FGV](https://blogdoibre.fgv.br/posts/expansao-mais-cara-do-credito-familias)
- **Pix parcelado, 2025:** after several postponements, in Dec 2025 the BCB board **decided not to create specific rules** for Pix Parcelado and **forbade institutions from using the name "Pix Parcelado"**. — [Agência Brasil, Dec 2025](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2025-12/apos-adiamentos-banco-central-desiste-de-regular-pix-parcelado)
- **Pix credit, Oct 2026:** BCB approved Resoluções BCB **601, 602 and 603** (dated 23 Oct 2026, published in the DOU on 26 Oct 2026; **effective 1 Jul 2027**). Res. 603 makes any credit offer at Pix initiation conditional on the payer's **prior express authorisation**, which takes effect **only after 24 hours**. Consent can be revoked at any time. The credit cannot exceed the Pix amount and cannot be offered on scheduled Pix or Pix Saque/Troco. **No interest cap and no maximum number of instalments.** Automatic alerts fire when instalments reach **40% of monthly payment capacity**, but new loans are not blocked. Res. 601 creates a **"Painel de Dívidas Consolidadas"** (consolidated debt dashboard). — [Jornal do Comércio, Oct 2026](https://www.jornaldocomercio.com/economia/2026/10/1268268-bc-define-regra-para-pix-no-credito-e-cria-alertas-contra-endividamento.html); [Atlas Público, Res. BCB 603/2026](https://atlaspublico.com.br/regulacao/bcb/resolucao-bcb-603-2026); [Bastidor Brasil](https://bastidorbrasil.com.br/m/pix-parcelado-ganha-regras-banco-central-exige-autorizacao-previa) (lower-quality outlet; details agree with the others)
- Banks already offer Pix-financed purchases on credit-card limits and personal-loan lines. — [Diário do Nordeste](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/negocios/bancos-oferecem-pix-parcelado-no-credito-e-no-emprestimo-pessoal-entenda-como-vai-funcionar-1.3686407)
- **Pix Automático** (Res. BCB 505/2025) became the mandatory tool for authorising and cancelling cross-bank automatic debits from **1 Jan 2026**. — [BLN Contabilidade](https://blncontabilidade.com.br/pix-automatico-empresas-como-funciona/) (secondary)
- **Crediário as a default driver:** CNDL/SPC Brasil (fieldwork 6-17 Mar 2026; n = 609 negativados) names card, loans and **crediário** as the main sources of default. **The card accounts for 42% of the overdue debts, up 18 percentage points vs 2025.** — [CNDL Varejo S.A., 2026](https://cndl.org.br/varejosa/cartao-de-credito-emprestimo-e-crediario-sao-os-principais-viloes-da-inadimplencia-no-pais-revela-pesquisa-cndl-spc-brasil-2/)

### Inferences
- **Game mechanic, "parcela pile-up":** each PSJ purchase adds a fixed slice to the next N faturas. Model a "comprometimento" gauge (sum of committed parcelas / monthly income) and warn the player at 30% (the national average is about 29%) and at 40% (the BCB's own new alert threshold for Pix credit).
- Pix-at-checkout credit is **not** interest-free. It is a personal loan or card financing, and the 2027 rules set no rate cap.

### Gaps
- **Crediário / carnê rates at Casas Bahia and Magalu:** no 2025-26 figures found. Both retailers' financial arms (Banqi / Casas Bahia carnê; Magalu's LuizaCred) publish CET on contracts, but I did not retrieve them.
- I found no reliable size figures for the Brazilian BNPL market.
- The specific "Pix Garantido" product history was not researched.

## 3. Other credit: cheque especial, empréstimo pessoal, consignado (incl. CLT 2025), FGTS, vehicle, informal / agiota, scams

### Takeaway
September 2026 BCB averages (PF, % a.a.): **cheque especial 139.0%**, **non-payroll personal loan 110.9%**, **consignado 28.5%**, **vehicles 26.2%**, **all free-market PF credit 62.4%**. Cheque especial is legally capped at **8% a.m. (~151.8% a.a.)**. Private-sector payroll loans ("Crédito do Trabalhador", launched March 2025) cost about 3.3-3.6% a.m., and their default rate is rising. The classic youth scam is the **advance-fee fake loan via WhatsApp**.

### Cited Findings
**BCB September 2026 rates (PF unless noted; prior month August in parentheses):** — all from [O Povo/Estadão, 29/10/2026](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2026/10/29/juro-medio-no-credito-livre-sobe-a-490-em-setembro-mostra-bc-cheque-especial-vai-a-139.html)
- Free-market credit, total: **49.0% a.a.** (48.5%; Sep 2025: 45.8%). PF: **62.4% a.a.** (61.8%). Companies: 24.4% a.a.
- **Cheque especial: 139.0% a.a.** (136.3%)
- **Personal loan, non-consignado: 110.9% a.a.** (110.4%)
- **Consignado (aggregate): 28.5% a.a.** (29.0%). No INSS / public / private breakdown in the article.
- **Vehicles: 26.2% a.a.** (26.4%)
- All credit (free + directed): 32.4% a.a.; ICC (average cost of the outstanding stock): 24.3%
- PF spread: **48.5 p.p.** (47.6)
- The article did not report Selic. I could not confirm the 2026 Selic level from an official source in this session.

**Cheque especial cap:**
- Since Resolução CMN 4.765/2019 (effective 2020) the cheque especial rate for individuals has been capped at **8% a.m.**. Procon-SP's survey found big banks charging exactly 8% a.m. in Sep 2026. — [SpaceMoney (Procon-SP survey)](https://www.spacemoney.com.br/credito/juros-emprestimo-pessoal-setembro); [Procon-SP rate survey, Jul 2026 (PDF)](https://www.procon.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RTTXJUROS-07.26.pdf)

**Personal loan, discrepancy between sources:**
- Procon-SP's survey of large banks: personal loan **8.23% → 8.66% a.m. in Sep 2026** (≈ 170% a.a., calc.). — [SpaceMoney](https://www.spacemoney.com.br/credito/juros-emprestimo-pessoal-setembro). **This conflicts with BCB's 110.9% a.a. (≈ 6.4% a.m., calc.).** The likely reason is that Procon-SP samples a handful of big banks' quoted rates, while BCB averages all contracted operations weighted by volume.

**Consignado CLT ("Crédito do Trabalhador", MP 1.292/2025, launched March 2025):**
- **R$1.28 bn** lent in the first 7 days. — [Folha PE](https://www.folhape.com.br/economia/credito-consignado-para-clt-tem-r-128-bilhao-em-emprestimos-nos/400795/)
- Rate: Ministry of Labour figures of **3.43% a.m. (May 2025) → 3.63% a.m. (June 2025)**. Annualised averages rose from about 44% a.a. (March) to about 59% a.a. (October; year implied 2025). — via search summary of [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/reportagem-exclusiva-fintechs/com-juros-em-alta-consignado-clt-passa-por-teste-de-inadimplencia/) and [Meutudo](https://meutudo.com.br/blog/noticias/2025/06/16/governo-pode-excluir-bancos-do-consignado-clt-por-juros-abusivos/) (secondary)
- Private consignado market average about **3.3% a.m. in Sep 2026** per BCB, as cited by a commercial lender. — [Creditas](https://www.creditas.com/exponencial/qual-banco-tem-a-menor-taxa-de-juros-para-emprestimo/) (commercial source; verify)
- **More than 40% of CLT consignado operations go to workers earning up to 2 minimum wages.** — [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/economia/consignado-clt-baixa-renda/)
- Delinquency: private consignado default reached **10% in July 2025** (highest in 15 years) per BCB data. — [TradeMap](https://trademap.com.br/noticias/news-8246984). Serasa found **one third of companies** in the programme had default episodes. — [Exame](https://exame.com/economia/consignado-clt-tem-inadimplencia-em-um-terco-das-empresas-diz-serasa/). A further figure of 4.9% → 6.6% (Nov 2025 → Mar 2026) appears in aggregator text. **Sources disagree on the level**, probably because they use different series (new-programme loans vs the whole private-consignado stock).

**Scams (golpes):**
- Serasa: **asking for money (a "taxa", insurance or tax) before releasing a loan is always a scam**. Scammers imitate institutions and promise easy credit "even for negativados". — [Serasa](https://www.serasa.com.br/credito/blog/golpe-do-emprestimo-tipos-mais-comuns-e-como-se-prevenir/)
- Febraban (Oct 2026): criminals use Febraban's logo on WhatsApp profiles to claim "suspicious transactions" and harvest account numbers and passwords. Febraban **does not offer loans or charge release fees**. — [Mercado&Consumo, 09/10/2026](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/09/10/2026/economia/febraban-alerta-que-criminosos-tem-usado-nome-da-entidade-para-aplicar-golpes/)
- Febraban's 2025 ranking: the **WhatsApp scam, fake sales and fake bank call centre / fake employee** were the main scams against bank customers in 2024. — [Movimento Econômico, Apr 2025](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/seguranca/2025/04/14/febraban-divulga-os-10-golpes-mais-aplicados-contra-clientes-bancarios/)
- Idec has flagged **unsolicited credit offers arriving by WhatsApp**. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/idec-na-imprensa/ofertas-de-credito-chegam-sem-solicitacao-por-whatsapp)

### Inferences
- Monthly equivalents (calc.) of the Sep 2026 BCB annual rates: cheque especial ≈ **7.5% a.m.** (just under the 8% cap; 8% a.m. = 151.8% a.a.); personal loan ≈ **6.4% a.m.**; free-market PF ≈ 4.1% a.m.; consignado ≈ 2.1% a.m.; vehicles ≈ 2.0% a.m.
- **A "credit ladder" for the game (cheapest to dearest, Sep 2026):** vehicle / consignado (~2% a.m.) < personal loan (~6-9% a.m.) < cheque especial (~7.5-8% a.m.) < card parcelado (~9.4% a.m.) < card rotativo (~15% a.m.). Agiotas sit off the top of the scale.
- Consignado is cheap because repayment is deducted from salary, but it locks part of the payslip. A teen's first CLT job (*jovem aprendiz*) could make them a target for these offers.

### Gaps
- **Agiota / informal lending rates and prevalence:** no reliable data found. Usury is a crime under Lei 1.521/1951 (background knowledge, not verified this session).
- **FGTS saque-aniversário anticipation loans:** rates and volumes not researched, apart from the Desenrola FGTS use (Section 4).
- Payday-style app lenders: no data found.
- No 2026 scam data broken down by age.

## 4. Debt consequences: negativação, score, Cadastro Positivo, Desenrola, superendividamento, statute of limitations, collectors

### Takeaway
**81.7 million Brazilians were negativados in Feb 2026** (Serasa), with an average debt of **R$6,598**. The 2026 **"Novo Desenrola" (Desenrola 2.0 / "Desenrola Famílias")** targets people earning up to 5 minimum wages who are 91-720 days overdue. Discounts reach up to 90%, interest is up to 1.99% a.m., up to 20% of the FGTS balance (or R$1,000) can be used, and participants are **banned from betting sites for a year**. A negative listing must be removed **5 years after the due date**. The debt itself does not vanish, and sources disagree on whether it can still be collected out of court.

### Cited Findings
**Negativação scale (Serasa Mapa da Inadimplência):**
- **February 2026: 81.7 million people in default**; **more than 332 million debts** (+43% vs 2016); **average debt R$6,598.13** per person (R$5,880.02 in 2016, inflation-adjusted, +12.2%). Defaulters have grown **38.1% in 10 years**. About **34 million** current defaulters were also in default 10 years ago. Women are 50.5% of defaulters. — [Serasa press release (10 years of the Map)](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/10-anos-do-mapa-de-inadimplencia/)
- **August 2026: 83.9 million** in default. — [Radium Web](https://www.radiumweb.com.br/post/inadimplencia-brasil-839-milhoes-serasa-agosto-2026) (secondary; I could not find the Serasa original)
- A separate SPC/CNDL count puts the figure at about **73 million** (Mar 2026). The methodology differs, so do not mix it with the Serasa series. — via search summary; see [CNDL](https://cndl.org.br/varejosa/cartao-de-credito-emprestimo-e-crediario-sao-os-principais-viloes-da-inadimplencia-no-pais-revela-pesquisa-cndl-spc-brasil-2/)

**Novo Desenrola Brasil (2026):**
- Eligibility: individuals with monthly income **up to 5 minimum wages**; credit contracts signed **by 31 Jan 2026**; instalments **91 to 720 days overdue** as of 03/05/2026. Eligible debts: **credit card, cheque especial, personal credit (CDC)**. Excluded, per one source: consignado and housing finance. — [Caixa, Novo Desenrola Brasil](https://www.caixa.gov.br/desenrola/Paginas/default.aspx) (search snippet; the page would not load when fetched); [Agência Brasil, May 2026](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2026-05/desenrola-20-entenda-como-usar-fgts-para-quitar-dividas)
- **FGTS:** optional. Up to **20% of the available balance or R$1,000, whichever is greater**, is paid by Caixa straight to the creditor. Available since **25 May 2026**. Using FGTS **suspends saque-aniversário** until the balance used is rebuilt. — [Agência Brasil, May 2026](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2026-05/desenrola-20-entenda-como-usar-fgts-para-quitar-dividas); [Serasa blog](https://www.serasa.com.br/limpa-nome-online/blog/desenrola-familias-como-usar-o-fgts-para-pagar-dividas/)
- Terms: **discounts of 30% to 90%**; **interest up to 1.99% a.m.** on the remaining balance; new-credit limit of **R$15,000 per institution**; **no betting (bets) for one year** after joining. — [Alerta Gov (aggregator)](https://alertagov.com.br/2026/05/novo-desenrola-brasil-guia-completo-para-renegociar-dividas-em-2026/); [Acerto](https://acerto.com.br/limpe-seu-nome/desenrola) (commercial aggregators; the official page could not be fetched to confirm)
- One source says the delay threshold is 90 days rather than 91. — noted in [Alerta Gov](https://alertagov.com.br/2026/05/quem-pode-participar-do-novo-desenrola-brasil-veja-requisitos/)

**Statute of limitations ("caducou"):**
- Serasa: **5 years after the debt's DUE DATE (not the listing date)**, the negative record must be removed and the debt no longer affects the score. — [Serasa Ensina, "dívida caduca"](https://www.serasa.com.br/ensina/seu-nome-limpo/divida-caduca/)
- Even after prescription, the debt **can keep appearing in Serasa Limpa Nome**, which is a negotiation marketplace, not the restrictive register. **A new agreement creates a new debt**: defaulting on it can restart a 5-year listing. — [Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/divida-prescreve-em-5-anos-mito-ou-verdade/)
- **Conflict:** many sources say prescription bars only lawsuits and that out-of-court collection may continue. A 2023 **STJ** decision, however, held that **no out-of-court collection** of a prescribed debt (calls, SMS, WhatsApp, or registry listing) is lawful. — [Conjur, 27/11/2023](https://www.conjur.com.br/2023-nov-27/stj-concede-perdao-ao-devedor-de-debito-prescrito//?print=1). Some secondary sites cite this as REsp 2.088.100 or 2.104.242; I have not checked the case number against the STJ.

**Lei do Superendividamento (Lei 14.181/2021):**
- The law amended the CDC to promote financial education and prevent and treat over-indebtedness, with emphasis on guaranteeing the **"mínimo existencial"**. — confirmed in search results ([Idec](https://idec.org.br/node/30262))

### Inferences
- **"Caducou" myth for the game:** after 5 years your name is cleaned and the score is no longer hit. The debt still exists, can be repaid voluntarily, and creditors may still deny you new credit. Signing a new agreement and then defaulting restarts the clock.
- Youth hook: Desenrola 2026 lets workers with FGTS (any CLT job, including *jovem aprendiz*) put part of it toward debt. That is a teachable trade-off against the saque-aniversário and the emergency value of FGTS.

### Gaps
- **Mínimo existencial value:** commonly cited as **R$600** (Decreto 11.150/2022, as amended by Decreto 11.567/2023). Not confirmed this session.
- **Serasa Score mechanics:** 0-1000 scale; drivers are paying bills on time, Cadastro Positivo history, keeping data updated, and not being negativado. This is background knowledge only; I fetched no source. The common myth that "checking your score lowers it" is untested here.
- **Cadastro Positivo:** automatic opt-in under LC 166/2019 (background knowledge; not verified this session).
- **Desenrola 1 (2023-24) results:** not retrieved. Widely reported as renegotiating tens of billions of reais and removing debts up to R$100 from registries, but unverified this session.
- **What collectors cannot do:** CDC art. 42 bars exposing the debtor to ridicule or threats, and art. 71 makes abusive collection a crime. Overcharged amounts are returned double (art. 42 §1). Not sourced this session.
- Credit bureaus: Serasa, SPC Brasil and Boa Vista (Equifax) all exist. Not researched further.

## 6. Disclosure concepts: CET, a.m. vs a.a., IOF, taxa vs CET

### Takeaway
Brazil quotes consumer loans **monthly (a.m.)**, while BCB statistics are published **annually (a.a.)**. Converting requires compounding, (1+i_m)^12 − 1, and the gap is huge at Brazilian rates. 15.3% a.m. is 452% a.a., not 184%. The CET (Custo Efetivo Total) bundles interest, fees, insurance and IOF. Fatura financing options must be ranked by CET.

### Cited Findings
- The BCB computes annual rates by extrapolating the monthly rate charged over 12 months, which explains the gap between the monthly and annual figures. — [WSCOM (BCB release coverage)](https://wscom.com.br/economia/2026/10/30/juro-do-rotativo-do-cartao-de-credito-sobe-para-4517-ao-ano-em-setembro-aponta-banco-central/) (snippet)
- Since 2024, card faturas must list financing options **ordered from lowest to highest total cost**, and show the charges that apply if only the minimum is paid. The 100% cap is computed **excluding IOF** (R$100 → max R$200 + IOF). — [O Povo, 22/12/2023](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2023/12/22/juros-do-rotativo-saiba-como-vai-funcionar-a-partir-de-2024.html)
- The Procon-SP monthly survey quotes rates **% a.m.** (personal loan 8.66% a.m., cheque especial 8% a.m., Sep 2026), showing the consumer-facing monthly convention. — [SpaceMoney / Procon-SP](https://www.spacemoney.com.br/credito/juros-emprestimo-pessoal-setembro)

### Inferences
- **Conversion table for the game (calc.):** 1% a.m. = 12.7% a.a.; 2% a.m. = 26.8%; 3% a.m. = 42.6%; 5% a.m. = 79.6%; 8% a.m. = 151.8%; 10% a.m. = 213.8%; 15% a.m. = 435.0%; 15.3% a.m. ≈ 452% a.a.
- **The "×12 trap":** naively multiplying 15% a.m. × 12 gives 180%, which understates the true 435% by a factor of about 2.4. This is a ready-made quiz moment.
- **Taxa vs CET:** a loan quoted at "2.99% a.m." can carry a higher CET once IOF, insurance (seguro prestamista) and fees are included. The game should always display both.

### Gaps
- **IOF rates on credit for individuals:** not confirmed this session. Background: a daily rate (0.0082% per day, capped at 365 days) plus an additional flat 0.38%. There were reports of IOF changes in 2025 (Decreto 12.466/2025 and related), so **the current 2026 values must be checked** against Receita Federal.
- The CET legal basis (CMN Res. 3.517/2007, now consolidated) was not verified this session.
