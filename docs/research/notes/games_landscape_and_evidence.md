# Financial-education games for Brazilian youth: competitive landscape and design-evidence brief (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: research done 2026-11-07 with about 25 web searches and fetches. Most Brazilian product pages were reached through press or secondary coverage, not opened live, so many "does it still exist in 2026" questions are flagged as gaps. Anything dated 2025 or earlier is marked that way.

## 1. Brazilian landscape: what exists, in what format, for whom, and is it alive?

### Takeaway
Brazil has plenty of institutional financial-education programmes: BCB Aprender Valor, the new MEC "Na Ponta do Lápis", Febraban Meu Bolso em Dia, B3 Bora Investir, Serasa Ensina, Junior Achievement and the Tesouro Direto simulators. What is missing is a good, free, no-login, Portuguese-language browser game for 14-25 year olds about credit-card rotativo, parcelamento and compound interest. The games that do exist are mostly quiz-gated (Febraban's Bate-Bola Financeiro), app-store-only and aimed at children (AEF-Brasil's Tá O$$O, JA's Meu Dinheiro Meu Negócio), investment-only competitions (B3 Desafio/HUB3, 18+), or board games (Estrela's Jogo da Vida and Jogo da Mesada). I found no Brazilian equivalent of NGPF's STAX, Spent or Shady Sam.

### Cited findings

**Banco Central do Brasil: Aprender Valor**
- Aprender Valor is a BCB programme for ensino fundamental. It is integrated into BNCC subjects (Maths, History, Geography, Portuguese) and delivered as "games, simulations and ready-made pedagogical projects" plus teacher training. Most projects can run without internet or technology. In its first five years it was public-schools-only and reached more than 24,000 schools in every state and about 58% of municipalities. No student count is given. — [Consumidor Moderno, 3 Jun 2025](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/banco-central-educacao-financeira-jovens/)
- In 2024 access widened to any educator with a gov.br silver or gold account, including private-school staff and NGOs. A 2025 platform rebuild opened private-school access via Gov.br. — [Consumidor Moderno 2025](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/banco-central-educacao-financeira-jovens/); [SP Secretaria da Educação document, Mar 2025](https://midiasstoragesec.blob.core.windows.net/001/2025/03/rede-156_educao-financeira--programa-aprender-valor-2025-plataforma-de-formao.pdf)
- In August 2025 the BCB announced that Aprender Valor would extend to public and private high schools (ensino médio) nationwide from 2026, with Anbima, CVM and Sebrae as partners. — [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/financas/bc-levar-educacao-financeira-estudantes-ensino-medio-todo-pais/); [Só Calculadora blog](https://socalculadora.com.br/blog/banco-central-amplia-educacao-financeira-ensino-medio)
- Teacher courses are free on Escola Virtual.Gov, with ENAP certificates; the teacher course is 40h. — [SP SEDUC Aprender Valor 2025 PDF](https://midiasstoragesec.blob.core.windows.net/001/2025/03/rede-156_educao-financeira--programa-aprender-valor-2025-plataforma-de-formao.pdf); [SP Atendimento KB](https://atendimento.educacao.sp.gov.br/knowledgebase/article/SED-08187/pt-br)
- Related: Olitef (Olimpíada do Tesouro Direto de Educação Financeira) awards R$100k infrastructure kits to public schools. The "Tesouros do Amanhã" olympiad partnership awarded R$100k in educational kits to 23 schools. — [Consumidor Moderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/banco-central-educacao-financeira-jovens/)
- Regulatory hook: Law 14.690/2023 gave the BCB and CMN direct responsibility for financial-education measures. Joint Resolution No. 8 (Dec 2023) requires financial institutions to keep financial-education policies for individuals. — [Consumidor Moderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/banco-central-educacao-financeira-jovens/)

**MEC: "Na Ponta do Lápis" and Pé-de-Meia**

The target is more than 30M students and 2M teachers. Adhesion by states and municipalities is voluntary, via a commitment term. — [MEC news, Jul 2025](https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/julho/mec-lanca-programa-de-educacao-financeira); [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202507/mec-lanca-programa-de-educacao-financeira-ensino-medio-fundamental); [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/educacao/na-ponta-do-lapis-mec-lanca-programa-de-educacao-financeira/)
- The Pé-de-Meia student-facing platform launched in Feb 2026 is a status and payment tracker on gov.br, not an education course. — [O Alagoano, 28 Feb 2026](https://oalagoano.com.br/noticias/2026/02/28/16210-mec-lanca-plataforma-para-estudantes-acompanharem-o-programa-pe-de-meia)

**Youth debt context (why rotativo matters)**
- Serasa: renegotiations by 18-25 year olds on Serasa Limpa Nome rose 49% from Jan to Jul 2025, to about 1.5M people, and their share rose from 9.9% to 13.3%. Gen Z is "the fastest-growing public in debt negotiations". — [Serasa press, Aug 2025](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/geracao-z-e-o-publico-que-mais-cresce-nas-negociacoes-de-dividas/); [Meu Tudo summary](https://meutudo.com.br/blog/noticias/2025/08/18/subiu-49-a-renegociacao-de-dividas-entre-jovens-de-18-a-25-anos/)
- 18-25s make up about 11-12% of all defaulters. Sources conflict on whether this group is the least or the most delinquent, probably because some measure share of total and others a within-group rate. Brazil had about 78M people in default in mid-2025. — [Serasa age-band PDF via Poder360](https://static.poder360.com.br/2025/08/serasa-faixa-etaria.pdf); [Paraíba Business](https://paraibabusiness.com.br/brasil-registra-78-milhoes-de-inadimplentes-aponta-pesquisa/)
- Gen Z led a 111% rise in credit-card applications. The date of this figure was not checked. — [Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/geracao-z-lidera-alta-de-111-nos-pedidos-de-cartao-de-credito/)
- **The rule the game must model correctly:** under Law 14.690/2023 (Desenrola) and CMN Resolution 5.112 (21 Dec 2023), total interest and charges on credit-card rotativo and fatura parcelamento cannot exceed 100% of the original debt (R$1,000 can grow to at most R$2,000). This has applied since 3 Jan 2024. The law caps the total amount, not the contracted rate. Before the cap, rotativo rates could reach about 430% a year. Sources disagree on whether the cap applies only to debts formed after 3 Jan 2024. IDEC criticised how vaguely "valor original da dívida" is defined. — [BMA Law](https://www.bmalaw.com.br/conteudo/mercados-financeiro-e-de-capitais/informativos-e-newsletters/nova-regra-sobre-limitacao-da-taxa-de-juros-do-credito-rotativo-entra-em-vigor/pdf); [O Povo, 3 Jan 2024](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2024/01/03/amp/juros-do-rotativo-novo-limite-entra-em-vigor-nesta-quarta-entenda.html); [Correio Braziliense](https://edicao.correiobraziliense.com.br/correiobraziliense/2024/01/03/20372c/pdf/A05-ECO-0301-DIG.pdf); [IDEC](https://idec.org.br/sites/default/files/contribuicoes_sobre_oferta_de_credito_e_endividamento_da_populacao.pdf)
- A Rico-linked portal gives the average rotativo rate as 451.5% a year (Aug 2025). This is a weak source, not confirmed against BCB series. — [RiConnect](https://riconnect.rico.com.vc/?p=681223)

### Inferences
- Gap in the market: no free Brazilian web game I found shows how a rotativo balance grows month by month, or the hidden cost of "parcelado sem juros" against an à-vista discount, and none lets a player feel compound growth over decades. The institutional players (BCB, Febraban, B3, Tesouro) offer calculators and simulators that require the user to already be motivated, which is the opposite of a game.
- The 100%-of-principal cap is a design opportunity and a correctness trap. A Brazilian game that shows rotativo compounding without limit would be factually wrong since 2024. The more accurate and more interesting story is the cap plus what happens around it: the forced switch to parcelamento, a negative Serasa record, losing the card, and the cheaper alternatives such as crédito pessoal or consignado that are still expensive.
- The most valuable partners for credibility and distribution appear to be the BCB (Aprender Valor's 2026 high-school expansion needs content), MEC "Na Ponta do Lápis" (needs Pé-de-Meia-linked materials) and Anbima (funds the high-school expansion and regulates finfluencers).
- Banks' and fintechs' presence in Roblox and Fortnite shows that teens are reached inside game platforms, but those are branded campaigns. A neutral, ad-free, open-source game can position against them as something schools can trust.

### Gaps
- I did not open the live 2026 pages for Bate-Bola Financeiro, Tá O$$O, Meu Dinheiro Meu Negócio, Duelo dos Investimentos or Jogo Vida Financeira, so I cannot confirm whether each still works or how many downloads it has. Google Play counts were not retrieved.
- I could not confirm whether Aprender Valor contains specific digital games, as opposed to offline "jogos" inside lesson projects, or what the 2026 high-school content contains.
- Not researched or not found: CVM's youth materials (the CVM Educacional portal), Anbima "Como Investir", Caixa, Nubank, Inter, PicPay, Rico and XP education content; Primo Rico's audience age; Instituto Ayrton Senna and Fundação Lemann financial-education work; "Dinheirama"; Brazilian itch.io titles. All of these remain gaps. Do not assume they lack games.
- No Serasa game was found; Serasa Ensina appears to be content and courses only.
- Pé-de-Meia: no dedicated education module or game was found, only the payment tracker and the umbrella "Na Ponta do Lápis".

## 2. International precedents worth borrowing from

### Takeaway
The most directly borrowable family is NGPF's free browser Arcade: STAX compresses 20 years of investing into about 20 minutes, Spent covers living paycheck to paycheck, Shady Sam casts the player as the loan shark, and every game ships with a reflection worksheet. Beyond that, idle and incremental game maths (Kongregate's Pecorella) is literally a compound-growth engine, and "Bad News" shows that a 15-minute browser role-reversal game can measurably inoculate players and can be localised.

### Cited findings
- NGPF Arcade has 11 free games as of Dec 2025 (12 in 2024), with "millions" of total plays. The 2025 top five were STAX! (investing, about 20 years compressed into a short session), Spent (living paycheck to paycheck), PAYBACK! (paying for college: debt, happiness, social life, academics; also in Spanish), Money Magic (budgeting to a savings goal) and Shady Sam (the player is a loan shark using predatory tactics to maximise profit). — [NGPF Best of 2025, 21 Dec 2025](https://dev5.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2025-most-played-arcade-games); [NGPF Best of 2024](https://dev7.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2024-most-played-arcade-games/)
- STAX is described as "Simulate 20 years of investing in 20 minutes!" and was the most-played game in both 2024 and 2025. Each game comes with a student reflection worksheet. — [NGPF blog](https://www.ngpf.org/blog/professional-development/boost-classroom-engagement-with-ngpf-arcade-games/)
- A third-party article says all NGPF games are free and browser-based as of 2026. — [Gerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/money-basics/ngpf-games-personal-finance)
- Universal Paperclips (Frank Lantz, NYU Game Center, Oct 2017) is free in the browser at decisionproblem.com/paperclips with no ads, sign-up or IAP, saves in the browser, and has a paid mobile port. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Paperclips); [Dinogame review](https://dinogame.gg/blog/universal-paperclips-review/)
- Idle-game maths (Anthony Pecorella, Kongregate; GDC Europe 2016 "Quest for Progress"): costs grow exponentially with the number owned while production grows linearly, which produces an early surplus that gives way to rising costs. Chains of generators, where each tier produces the next, approach exponential growth. Prestige resets let players push further. Pecorella published spreadsheet models with the slides. — [Game Developer, Math of Idle Games pt I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i); [pt II](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/the-math-of-idle-games-part-ii); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1023876/Quest-for-Progress-The-Math); [SlideShare deck](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/quest-for-progress-gdc-europe-2016/65405507); [GameAnalytics](https://gameanalytics.com/?p=6634)
- Bad News (getbadnews.com, free browser) casts players as fake-news producers learning six techniques. In the 2019 Palgrave Communications study (about 15,000 players), perceived reliability of fake headlines fell by an average of 21% after play, with no change in how real news was rated, and the biggest gains went to the most susceptible players. A Royal Society Open Science follow-up found effects lasting up to 13 weeks with "booster" reminders, similar across language versions. About 15 minutes of play gave a "moderate" effect. — [Cambridge news](https://cam.ac.uk/node/206062); [HKS Misinformation Review](https://misinforeview.hks.harvard.edu/article/global-vaccination-badnews/); [Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/prebunking-disinformation-14314/amp/)

### Inferences
- Shady Sam and Bad News both use role reversal (you play the predator). That is the strongest non-preachy way to show a debt trap: a teen who designs a "parcelado em 12x sem juros" offer, or a rotativo minimum-payment screen, learns to spot the tactic. Bad News's cross-language effects suggest the mechanic would survive localisation into Brazilian Portuguese.
- STAX's "20 years in 20 minutes" is direct precedent for compressing a 30-year horizon into a single class period.
- An idle or incremental structure maps naturally onto compound interest. The exponential cost and linear production seesaw can be inverted: debt is the exponential cost and wage is the linear production, while investment can become a generator chain.

### Gaps
- The licences and source availability of NGPF games were not verified, and NGPF games are probably proprietary and free-to-use rather than open source. The licence for Universal Paperclips was not checked; it is closed source as far as known.
- Not verified this session (do not cite without checking): Spent's maker (believed to be McKinney for Urban Ministries of Durham, 2011); Visa's Practical Money Skills and Financial Football; Zogo; Greenlight; Cashflow 101; The Uber Game (FT, 2017); Lemonade Stand; BitLife; Alter Ego; Reigns; 80 Days; Papers Please; Cookie Clicker and AdVenture Capitalist specifics. All are omitted for lack of sourcing in this session.
- No GitHub search was done for open-source financial-literacy games or their licences.

## 3. Evidence: what changes financial behaviour, and what Brazilian teens get wrong

### Takeaway
The evidence is mixed but positive. A large 2022 meta-analysis of 76 RCTs finds real effects on knowledge and behaviour, while the earlier Fernandes et al. (2014) meta-analysis finds tiny effects that decay within about 20 months, which argues for "just-in-time" education tied to specific decisions. The best Brazilian evidence, the Bruhn et al. school RCT with about 20,000 students, raised saving and budgeting but also raised use of expensive credit: knowledge alone can make teens more confident borrowers. Brazil's PISA 2022 financial-literacy mean was 416 against an OECD average of 498, with 45% of students below baseline. Exponential growth bias is real, widespread and comes with overconfidence, which is exactly the misconception a game should target.

### Cited findings
- **Kaiser, Lusardi, Menkhoff & Urban (2022, J. Financial Economics 145:255-272):** 76 RCTs, more than 160,000 people, 33 countries. They find positive causal effects on financial knowledge and on downstream behaviours such as saving. Effects are at least three times the average in earlier work, comparable to interventions in other education fields, and robust to publication-bias correction and to restricting to adequately powered studies. — [RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:145:y:2022:i:2:p:255-272); [DIW discussion paper](https://www.diw.de/de/diw_01.c.785790.de/publikationen/diskussionspapiere/2020_1864/financial_education_affects_financial_knowledge_and_downstream_behaviors.html); [GFLEC/NEFE summary](https://gflec.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Meta_analysis_LusardiUrban-NEFE-final.pdf)
- **Fernandes, Lynch & Netemeyer (2014, Management Science 60(8):1861-1883):** interventions explain only 0.1% of the variance in financial behaviours, with weaker effects in low-income samples. Even large interventions have negligible effects 20 or more months later. The authors recommend "just-in-time" financial education tied to specific behaviours. The study count varies by version: 201 studies and 585,168 participants in the published version, 188 in the draft. — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2333898); [NEFE digest](https://nefe.org/news/nefe-digest/2014/time-for-financial-education-to-evolve.aspx); [RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormnsc:v:60:y:2014:i:8:p:1861-1883)
- **Bruhn, Leão, Legovini, Marchetti & Zia (2016, AEJ: Applied 8(4):256-95; WB WP 6723):** a randomised evaluation in 6 states, 868 schools and about 20,000 high-school students (replication data lists 892 schools and about 25,000 students). Financial proficiency rose about 0.25 SD and grade-passing rates improved. Saving and budgeting improved, with positive spillovers to parents' knowledge, savings and spending, but students also increased their use of expensive credit for consumer purchases. The working paper reports a 1.4 pp increase in saving for purchases. — [IDEAS/RePEc AEJ](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aejapp/v8y2016i4p256-95.html); [World Bank OKR](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/c5c6d6cc-78aa-5539-bc83-a71dd8feca4f/full); [openICPSR](https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/project/116339/version/V1/view); [RePEc WP 6723](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:wbk:wbrwps:6723)
- **PISA 2022 financial literacy, Brazil:** mean 416 against an average of 498 across the 14 participating OECD systems; 20 systems took part. Brazil is unchanged from 2018 and 22 points above 2015. 45% of Brazilian students are below Level 2 (OECD 18%) and about 2% reach Level 5 (OECD 11%). The socioeconomic gap is 86 points (OECD 87). — [OECD PISA 2022 Vol IV Brazil factsheet](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-iv-factsheets_34d60137-en/brazil_1c815ef9-en.html); [OECD Vol IV ch.](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-iv_5a849c2a-en/full-report/component-5.html)
- PISA 2018: Brazil's financial-literacy mean was 420, cited as 17th of the participating systems. — [UFC repository, citing OECD 2019](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/83222?locale=es)
- PISA 2022 core domains for Brazil: maths 379, reading 410, science 403 (OECD 472/476/485). — [Itaú Social](https://www.itausocial.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/PISA-2022-contextualizacao-e-perspectivas.pdf)
- **Exponential growth bias (EGB):** Stango & Zinman (2009, J. Finance) show that people underestimate interest rates implied by loan terms and future values of investments. More-biased households had higher short-term debt to income, held less stock and had lower net worth (SCF 1977/1983, correlational). Enforced APR disclosure compressed the rates paid by more- and less-biased borrowers. — [RePEc J Finance 2009](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:64:y:2009:i:6:p:2807-2849); [CGU EGB paper](https://research.cgu.edu/dpe-events-and-conferences/wp-content/uploads/sites/37/2016/12/EGB.pdf)
- Levy & Tasoff find substantial EGB in experiments and, more importantly, overconfidence in one's ability to answer exponential-growth questions. They also note that Stango-Zinman is correlational. — [Levy & Tasoff, LSE](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/levymr/papers/LevyTasoff.pdf); [EGB overconfidence paper](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/levymr/papers/EGBOC.pdf)
- Foltice & Langer report encouraging evidence that teaching German university students the savings-growth formula reduces bias (summary only). A Colorado College thesis studies how exposure and experience affect EGB. — [CGU EGB PDF](https://research.cgu.edu/dpe-events-and-conferences/wp-content/uploads/sites/37/2016/12/EGB.pdf); [Colorado College](https://digitalcc.coloradocollege.edu/record/1511/files/Variable%20Effects%20of%20Exposure%20and%20Experience%20on%20Future%20Exponential%20Growth%20Bias.pdf)
- **Game-based finance RCT:** an online game-based course ("Financial Escape Room") was tested in a school-level RCT with 2,220 students in four countries. It raised financial literacy by 0.313 SD (J. Comparative Economics, 2024). — [Maastricht CRIS](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/the-impact-of-an-online-game-based-financial-education-course-mul/); [HvA](https://research.hva.nl/en/publications/the-impact-of-an-online-game-based-financial-education-course-mul/)
- **Moonshot** (Konstanz; quasi-experimental 2×2, n=293 upper-secondary students): strategic game mechanics combined with direct reflection prompts significantly increased students' perceived utility value of the game. Financial interest showed only a group-independent time effect. — [MDPI Education Sciences 15(2):227](https://mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/2/227); [Uni Konstanz project](https://wiwi.uni-konstanz.de/fr/schumann/research/research-projects/current-projects/game-based-learning-and-financial-literacy)
- The Stock Market Game: a 2008 RCT in about 600 classrooms found higher financial-literacy scores. It was sponsor-funded (FINRA Foundation / SIFMA), so treat with caution. — [SIFMA study summary](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/education_sifma-foundation_learning-point-study-summary.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Bruhn et al. result, more saving but also more expensive credit, is the single most important design warning for a Brazilian game. Teaching "how credit works" without making its cost felt may raise borrowing confidence. The game should make the cost of rotativo and long parcelamento visceral, not just explain it.
- Because of decay (Fernandes et al.) and the "booster" result (Bad News), the game should be short, replayable and re-encounterable close to real decisions: a first card, a first salary, Black Friday parcelamento, or receiving a Pé-de-Meia deposit. That is the "just-in-time" window.
- EGB comes with overconfidence (Levy & Tasoff). A strong mechanic is "predict, then reveal": ask the player to guess the balance in month 12 or year 30 before showing it. This exposes the gap between guess and outcome, which is the precondition for debiasing.
- Both the escape-room RCT (0.31 SD) and Moonshot point to game plus reflection as the effective unit. That matches NGPF's worksheet model and argues for a built-in debrief screen and teacher prompts.

### Gaps
- The PISA 2022 item-level detail on what Brazilian teens get wrong (for example interest, inflation, contracts, digital fraud) was not retrieved. The OECD Vol IV full report has item-type breakdowns and should be checked.
- I did not read Kaiser & Menkhoff (2017, World Bank Econ Review) directly. Its known finding, that effects are smaller for low-income groups and for borrowing behaviour, is not verified this session.
- The Goda et al. (2019) link between EGB and high-interest debt is cited secondhand only.
- No RCT on idle or incremental mechanics for teaching compound interest was found.

## 4. Game design patterns for this genre

### Takeaway
Sourced precedent supports: time compression through month or year ticks (STAX's 20 years in 20 minutes), exponential cost against linear income (idle maths), role reversal so the player learns a debt trap without being preached at (Shady Sam, Bad News), short sessions of about 15 minutes with replays and boosters, and a reflection step after play. Brazil-specific tone, accessibility and teacher needs below are mostly inference, as little primary sourcing was found.

### Cited findings
- Time compression: STAX covers about 20 years of investing in about 20 minutes and is NGPF's most-played game in 2024 and 2025. — [NGPF](https://dev5.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2025-most-played-arcade-games)
- Exponential cost with linear production as the core tension; generator chains approach exponential growth; prestige resets extend the curve. — [Game Developer pt I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i); [pt II](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/the-math-of-idle-games-part-ii)
- Role reversal (playing the predatory lender, or the manipulator) works in a free browser format in about 15 minutes. — [NGPF Shady Sam](https://dev5.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2025-most-played-arcade-games); [Cambridge on Bad News](https://cam.ac.uk/node/206062)
- Reflection prompts combined with strategic mechanics raise perceived value. — [MDPI Moonshot](https://mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/2/227)
- Paycheck-to-paycheck simulation (Spent) is NGPF's #2 game, so "survive the month" one-decision loops are proven in classrooms. — [NGPF](https://dev5.ngpf.org/blog/best-of/best-of-2025-most-played-arcade-games)
- Universal Paperclips shows that a no-login, no-ads, browser-saved, text-light incremental game can reach a mass audience. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Paperclips)

### Inferences (design recommendations, not sourced facts)
- **Tick structure:** use monthly ticks for credit (a bill arrives, choose to pay in full, pay the minimum, or parcelar) and switch to yearly fast-forward for investing and career. That gives a 30-year arc in about 15 minutes, with a STAX-style compression mode.
- **Debt felt, not preached:** give the rotativo balance a visible "fuse", and show the 100% cap reached and its consequences (Serasa record, losing the card, offers of crédito pessoal or consignado). Include "parcelado sem juros" against an à-vista discount as an implicit-interest puzzle. A Shady Sam-style mode where the player designs the store's 12x offer would add role reversal.
- **Predict-then-reveal** before each fast-forward, to counter EGB overconfidence.
- **Win conditions:** use several outcomes (wealth, wellbeing, free time, family obligations), as Payback and Jogo da Vida do, rather than "most money wins". Jogo da Vida's "most money wins" is the anti-pattern.
- **Characters and backgrounds for empathy:** a Pé-de-Meia recipient in the Northeast, a CLT worker in SP, a gig worker (app delivery), a university student on FIES. This matters because Brazil's PISA socioeconomic gap is 86 points.
- **Tone:** Brazilian meme and WhatsApp humour, with "golpe do Pix"-style scam events as inoculation moments (this extends the Bad News evidence to finance; untested).
- **Classroom constraints:** no login, works offline after load (Aprender Valor stresses projects that run without internet), mobile-first on low-end Android, and one class period.
- **Avoid dark patterns:** no streak shaming, loot boxes or ads, given the critique that games and digital culture are speeding up youth debt.

### Gaps
- No sourced material was found on Brazilian youth humour and tone in edu-games, on accessibility standards for Brazilian edu-games (e.g. eMAG or WCAG adoption), or on what Brazilian teachers say they need from a game. No GDC or Lost Garden or Raph Koster serious-game pieces were retrieved beyond the idle-maths series.

## 5. Distribution channels in Brazil

### Takeaway
The channels are real and newly expanding in 2025-2026: Aprender Valor's high-school rollout from 2026, MEC's "Na Ponta do Lápis" targeting 30M students and Pé-de-Meia recipients, Semana ENEF every May, state secretariats (SP actively pushes Aprender Valor), Plataforma MEC RED and eduCAPES as repositories, and finfluencers with 310M+ combined followers. Implementation in schools is uneven: educação financeira is a BNCC "Tema Contemporâneo Transversal" with fuzzy obligation and weak management support, so ready-to-use, no-login, BNCC-mapped material with a lesson plan is what lowers the barrier.

### Cited findings
- BNCC treats educação financeira as a Tema Contemporâneo Transversal to be addressed across subjects. Sources conflict on whether it is mandatory or optional in practice. — [Ferreira 2022 (funes)](https://funes.uniandes.edu.co/wp-content/uploads/tainacan-items/32454/1206047/Ferreira2022A.pdf); [PUC-SP EMD](https://revistas.pucsp.br/index.php/emd/article/download/65730/48661/246841)
- Research describes school financial education in Brazil as still "in development", calls for more evaluation of its effectiveness, and identifies education management as an obstacle in public schools. — [PUC-SP](https://revistas.pucsp.br/index.php/emd/article/download/65730/48661/246841); [UECE BOCEHM](https://revistas.uece.br/index.php/BOCEHM/article/download/12996/12263)
- The 12th Semana ENEF ran 12-18 May 2025 with a central theme of financial education for children and young people. It is organised by the Fórum Brasileiro de Educação Financeira (FBEF), whose 2025/2026 priorities include "Educação Financeira nas escolas". — [Mundo Coop](https://mundocoop.com.br/economia-negocios/12a-semana-nacional-de-educacao-financeira-ja-tem-data-definida/); [gov.br Semana ENEF 2025 summary](https://www.gov.br/semanaenef/pt-br/sobre/Apresentaoresumo12Senef2025.pdf)
- MEC "Na Ponta do Lápis" (Jul 2025): voluntary state and municipal adhesion, teacher training, Pé-de-Meia focus, target 30M students and 2M teachers. — [MEC](https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/julho/mec-lanca-programa-de-educacao-financeira)
- Aprender Valor will expand to ensino médio from 2026, with Anbima, CVM and Sebrae. São Paulo's SEDUC publishes Aprender Valor training guidance for its network. — [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/financas/bc-levar-educacao-financeira-estudantes-ensino-medio-todo-pais/); [SP SEDUC 2025](https://midiasstoragesec.blob.core.windows.net/001/2025/03/rede-156_educao-financeira--programa-aprender-valor-2025-plataforma-de-formao.pdf)
- Plataforma MEC RED hosts more than 300k digital resources (games, PDFs, lesson plans, apps). Its licence is listed as "Other". One study found 70.8% of sampled resources had no licence information and only 18% had full metadata; the platform code is free but the content is not fully open. eduCAPES hosts some CC-licensed Brazilian financial-education games (one cooperative-finance game; one CC0 financial-maths gamification resource). — [OE Week listing](https://oeweek.oeglobal.org/resources/2019/plataforma-integrada-mec-de-recursos-educacionais-digitais-mec-red/); [SBC/CBIE study](https://sol.sbc.org.br/index.php/cbie_estendido/article/view/31783); [eduCAPES item](https://educapes.capes.gov.br/handle/capes/602476?mode=full); [eduCAPES item 2](https://educapes.capes.gov.br/handle/capes/431409?mode=full)
- Finfluencer reach: 904 monitored creators, 310.7M followers and about 1.3bn interactions per semester (2H 2025). Instagram is the main channel. Anbima rules on paid finfluencer advertising date from 2023. — [Anbima](https://www.anbima.com.br/pt_br/noticias/audiencia-de-finfluencers-cresce-mais-de-300-e-ultrapassa-310-milhoes-nas-redes.htm)
- Global Money Week runs through B3 / Bora Investir. — [Bora Investir GMW](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/b3/gmw/)

### Inferences
- A free, open-source (e.g. MIT code plus CC BY content), no-login browser game would stand out on MEC RED and eduCAPES precisely because most resources there lack clear licences. Clear CC licensing plus full metadata is a cheap differentiator.
- The likely adoption path is to launch around Semana ENEF (May 2027), pitch for inclusion in the Aprender Valor ensino médio content and in Na Ponta do Lápis teacher training, and ship a teacher kit: a one-page BNCC mapping (Matemática EM13MAT skills on juros compostos; TCT Educação Financeira), a 50-minute lesson plan and a reflection worksheet, following the NGPF model.
- Finfluencers are the best out-of-school channel. A shareable end-of-game "your 30-year result" card fits Instagram and TikTok. Note Anbima's rules if any financial institution sponsors creators.
- Pé-de-Meia recipients (low-income ensino-médio students receiving deposits) are a natural "just-in-time" audience: the game could model what happens to the Pé-de-Meia savings deposit.

### Gaps
- No state-level curricula were verified (MG, PR, CE financial-education disciplines or electives), apart from SP's Aprender Valor guidance and JA Ceará's elective (2022).
- Exact BNCC skill codes for financial mathematics were not retrieved from the official BNCC text.
- Google Play "Teacher Approved" and Families policies, and whether they apply to Brazil, were not researched.
- No data was found on how many Brazilian teachers actually use digital games in financial-education lessons, or on device and connectivity limits in public schools (TIC Educação / Cetic.br would be the source).
