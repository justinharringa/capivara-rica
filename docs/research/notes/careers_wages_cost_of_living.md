## 1. Wages: minimum wage, income by education/age/region/formality, starting salaries for common jobs

### Takeaway
The 2026 salário mínimo is **R$ 1.621/month** (R$ 7,37/hour), up 6,79% from R$ 1.518 in 2025. It is indexed to INPC plus GDP growth from two years earlier, with the real gain capped at 2,5%. The national average income from work is roughly 2× the minimum. A degree roughly doubles to 2.5× pay versus ensino médio: about R$ 5.800 for superior completo against about R$ 2.300–3.000 for médio. Regional gaps are large: work income in the Nordeste averages R$ 2.475, against about R$ 4.000+ in the South, Southeast and Centre-West.

### Cited Findings
**Minimum wage and indexation**
- 2026 minimum wage **R$ 1.621**, a 6,79% (R$ 103) rise over **R$ 1.518 (2025)**, in force from 1 Jan 2026 and first paid on February payslips. The formula is INPC for the 12 months to November of the prior year (4,18%) plus GDP growth from two years earlier, limited to between 0,6% and 2,5% real (2024 GDP was 3,4%, so it was capped at 2,5%). The raw result of R$ 1.620,99 was rounded to R$ 1.621. — [Band, Dec 2025](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/valor-salario-minimo-2026-reajuste-r-1621-202512101209); [B3 Bora Investir](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/inflacao-que-reajustara-o-salario-minimo-fecha-em-418-confira-o-valor-para-2026/); [Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/noticia/311858-salario-minimo-para-2026-e-fixado-em-r-1621-com-reajuste-de-679)
- The PLDO 2026 had projected R$ 1.627; that figure was not adopted. — [Band](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/valor-salario-minimo-2026-reajuste-r-1621-202512101209)
- Hourly minimum wage 2026 is **R$ 7,37** (1.621/220 h; Decreto nº 12.797/2025). — [Genyo](https://genyo.com.br/jovem-aprendiz/)
- Sept 2025 minimum was R$ 1.518 (DIEESE bulletin). The 2024 value is not in my sources; it was R$ 1.412 **[unverified, from prior knowledge]**. — [DIEESE Sept 2026 bulletin](https://www.dieese.org.br/analisecestabasica/2026/202609cestabasica.pdf)
- 2027 projection: a Sept 2026 article cites a projected **R$ 1.741** minimum wage for 2027 (headline). This is a projection, not law. — [Sociedade Militar, Sept 2026](https://www.sociedademilitar.com.br/2026/09/r-1-741-x-r-1-177-salario-minimo-deve-subir-em-2027-enquanto-soldo-do-recruta-fica-r-564-abaixo-e-militares-entram-no-ano-sem-nova-parcela-prevista-afch.html)

### Inferences
- A suggested game ladder (monthly gross, 2026): aprendiz R$ 737–1.106, then caixa/atendente/telemarketing at about the minimum wage (R$ 1.621–2.000), then auxiliar administrativo ~R$ 2.200. Entregador nets about R$ 1.900–3.000 but carries high cost and risk. Técnico de enfermagem is R$ 3.325 after a 2-year técnico course. Dev júnior is R$ 3.500 typical, up to R$ 6.800 at top firms. Enfermeiro (degree) is R$ 4.750+; professor 40 h is R$ 5.131; a nível-médio concurso is R$ 4.200–6.000 with stability.
- Regional multiplier for the game: Nordeste ≈ 0,62× and Sudeste ≈ 1,0× (2.475/3.958 from IBGE work income).

## 2. Formal vs informal: payslip deductions, MEI, gig economy

### Takeaway
A CLT teen on the minimum wage loses 7,5% INSS (R$ 121,57). They pay no IRRF, and income up to R$ 5.000 is fully exempt from 2026. They lose up to 6% for vale-transporte if they take it. The employer deposits 8% FGTS (R$ 129,68) on top. An MEI pays a flat ~R$ 82–87/month DAS and can bill up to R$ 81 mil/year.

### Cited Findings
- **INSS 2026 (employee) progressive table**: up to R$ 1.621,00 → 7,5%; R$ 1.621,01–2.902,84 → 9%; R$ 2.902,85–4.354,27 → 12%; R$ 4.354,28–8.475,55 → 14%. Ceiling R$ 8.475,55; maximum deduction ~R$ 988,09/month (Portaria Interministerial MPS/MF nº 13, 9 Jan 2026). Individual contributors pay a flat 11%. — [Valor Final](https://valorfinal.com.br/tabela-inss-2026); [Trivium Contábil](https://triviumcontabil.com.br/tabela-inss-2026/); [Marra CLT](https://www.marraclt.com.br/salario/teto-inss-2026-tabela-aliquotas-desconto-folha); [Contabilizei](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/contabilidade-online/tabela-inss/)
- **IRRF 2026 (Lei 15.270/2025)**: a monthly reduction of up to **R$ 312,89 zeroes the tax for gross income up to R$ 5.000**. For R$ 5.000,01–7.350 the reduction is **R$ 978,62 − 0,133145 × income**, phasing out at R$ 7.350. Example: at R$ 6.000 the reduction is R$ 179,75. Above R$ 7.350 the normal progressive table applies. It also applies to the 13º. People with several income sources benefit only if the total stays ≤ R$ 5 mil. — [contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/irrf-2026-como-calcular-o-imposto-de-renda-retido-na-fonte-com-a-nova-tabela-e-reducao-ate-r-7-350/); [Band, Jan 2026](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/veja-a-tabela-de-isencao-e-reducao-do-ir-mensal-em-2026-202601201115); [Meu Tudo](https://meutudo.com.br/blog/noticias/2026/01/07/faixas-e-aliquotas-sao-atualizadas-na-nova-tabela-do-imposto-de-renda-2026/)
- **MEI 2026**: DAS = 5% of the minimum wage for INSS (R$ 81,05), plus R$ 1 ICMS (commerce/industry) and/or R$ 5 ISS (services). That gives **R$ 82,05 (commerce), R$ 86,05 (services), R$ 87,05 (both)**. MEI caminhoneiro pays R$ 195,52–200,52. **Limit R$ 81.000/year (~R$ 6.750/month)**. — [O Povo, Jan 2026](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2026/01/05/mei-vai-pagar-mais-em-2026-contribuicao-mensal-sobe-para-rs-8105.html); [Genyo](https://genyo.com.br/teto-mei-anual/)
- Conflict: one source (TabNews) says LC 238/2026 raises the MEI ceiling to R$ 110 mil in 2027 and R$ 140 mil from 2028. Omie (Sept 2026) says the limit is unchanged pending PLP 67/2025. **Unresolved.** — [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/cairolabs/calculando-o-limite-do-mei-e-o-das-de-2026-em-30-linhas-de-javascript-com-o-que-muda-pela-lc-238-2026); [Omie](https://www.omie.com.br/blog/mei-faturamento-anual-voce-sabe-qual-e/index.md)
- Gig costs: Cebrap estimates ~R$ 700/month equipment and vehicle maintenance for couriers, and net ~R$ 23/h vs gross ~R$ 29,65/h. — [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/veja-quem-sao-e-quanto-ganham-os-entregadores-por-aplicativo-no-brasil/)

- IRRF is zero for every salary in the youth game range up to R$ 5.000. That makes INSS + VT the only payslip deductions a beginner sees.
- Standard CLT benefits **[unverified law, not re-sourced this session]**: 13º salário (one extra monthly salary in Nov/Dec, Lei 4.090/62); 30 days' férias + 1/3 bonus (CF art. 7º XVII); vale-transporte with employee share capped at 6% of base salary (Lei 7.418/85); vale-refeição is not mandatory except by collective agreement (PAT). So CLT annual gross ≈ 13,33 × monthly, plus FGTS ≈ 8% × 13,33. An MEI/PJ earning the same monthly figure gets none of this. As a game rule, PJ needs roughly a 30–40% higher monthly figure to break even (inference).
- Informal workers pay no INSS and so have no sick leave or seguro-desemprego. That makes them a good game "risk" flag.

## 3. Youth labour facts

### Takeaway
Youth unemployment hit series lows in 2025–26: 18–24 at **11,4% in Q4 2025** and **13,8% in Q1 2026** (seasonal), against a national 5,4% in Q2 2026. About **17,5% of 15–29 year-olds are "nem-nem"**, 8,1 million people, and women are nearly twice as affected (22,8% vs 12,4%). Aprendiz and estágio rules cap hours at 6 h/day for most young people.

### Cited Findings
- Unemployment 18–24: **12,8% (Q4 2024) → 11,4% (Q4 2025)**, the lowest since 2012. — [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/desemprego-entre-os-jovens-cai-em-2025/)
- Q1 2026: 18–24 at **13,8%**; 14–17 at **25,1%**; about 2,4× the national average. — [O Imparcial, Jun 2026](https://oimparcial.com.br/noticias/2026/06/desemprego-entre-jovens-cai-para-138-mas-baixa-remuneracao-e-rotatividade-sao-desafios/)
- Q2 2026: youth (14–29) unemployment **10,0%**, the lowest in the series. — [DIEESE Boletim Emprego em Pauta 34](https://www.dieese.org.br/boletimempregoempauta/2026/boletimEmpregoPauta34.pdf)
- National unemployment **5,4% in Q2 2026**, and 5,3% for May–Jul 2026. Women's unemployment is 39% higher than men's (Q2 2026). — [Movimento Econômico, Jul 2026](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/trabalho/2026/07/30/desemprego-no-brasil-cai-para-54-e-atinge-menor-nivel-desde-2012-aponta-ibge/); [Band](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/desemprego-de-mulheres-e-39-maior-que-de-homens-no-2-tri26-202608141003)

- **Nem-nem** (IBGE SIS, released Jun 2026, data 2025): **8,1 milhões** people aged 15–29 neither working, studying nor in a qualification course, about **17,5%**, the lowest in 7 years. Women 22,8%, men 12,4%. The definition changed (it now excludes those in qualification courses), so it is not comparable with 22,3% (2022) or 21,2% (2023). Other press cites 6,2 milhões nem-nem on a different age cut. — [Diario de Pernambuco, Jun 2026](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/brasil/2026/06/11716952-n-de-jovens-nem-nem-no-brasil-cai-mas-ainda-e-maior-que-populacao-de-santa-catarina.html); [A Gazeta ES](https://www.agazeta.com.br/es/economia/cai-o-numero-de-jovens-que-nao-trabalham-e-nao-estudam-no-es-0626); [Acessa, Jun 2026](https://www.acessa.com/economia/2026/06/332051-jovens-ocupados-sao-a-maioria-mas-62-milhoes-seguem-como-nem-nem.html)

### Inferences

## 5. Education paths: costs and returns

### Takeaway
Education has a large payoff in the data. Unemployment is **9,0% with ensino médio incompleto vs 3,0% with superior completo** (Q2 2026). A degree pays ~2,3–2,6× ensino médio in raw averages, and ~25%+ in causal estimates. Private tuition is now cheap: median presencial R$ 835/month and EAD R$ 214/month in 2026. Public options are free (SISU: 274,8 mil places in 2026), and ProUni and FIES lower the barrier. Pé-de-Meia pays up to R$ 9.200 over 3 years of public ensino médio. Dropout is the main risk: ~58% presencial and 63% EAD over 2015–2024.

### Cited Findings

- FIES juros zero with the repayment cap of 10% of income makes the debt low-risk: on R$ 3.000 the instalment is ≤ R$ 300.

## 6. Life shocks and milestones (game events)

### Takeaway
Phone theft is the most frequent documented shock. São Paulo city lost **161.666 phones in 2025 (~442/day)**, and Brazil averaged ~95/hour. Bets are a documented drain: households lost **R$ 62,5 bi in 2025**, and the youngest bettors average ~R$ 100/month (BC, 2024). Fare increases happen every January (+6–20%). Teen births are falling but still number hundreds of thousands per year.
