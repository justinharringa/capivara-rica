## 1. Credit-card minimum payment, rotativo cycle, and the 100% cap

### Takeaway
The 100% cap has applied since 3 Jan 2024 (some reports say 2 Jan). Total juros + encargos on rotativo and fatura instalments cannot exceed the original debt. IOF is excluded from the cap. The cap counts from the first day of rotativo, and moving to parcelamento does not restart it. This pass did not re-confirm the minimum-payment percentage or the 30-day rotativo rule (see [MEMORY] items).

### Cited Findings
- Lei 14.690/2023 and Res. CMN 5.112/2023 limit total juros and encargos on rotativo and fatura instalments to the original debt amount. Example: a R$1,000 debt can accrue at most R$1,000 in juros/encargos, so at most R$2,000 in total. The "original amount" is the balance of the rotativo or parcelamento operation. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/dicas-e-direitos/teto-de-juros-do-cartao-de-credito-conheca-mudancas-nos-limites-do-rotativo); [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/regulamentacao/juro-do-rotativo-do-cartao-de-credito-sera-limitado-a-100/)
- **IOF is excluded** from the 100% cap calculation. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/dicas-e-direitos/teto-de-juros-do-cartao-de-credito-conheca-mudancas-nos-limites-do-rotativo); [BMA Advogados](https://www.bmalaw.com.br/conteudo/mercados-financeiro-e-de-capitais/informativos-e-newsletters/nova-regra-sobre-limitacao-da-taxa-de-juros-do-credito-rotativo-entra-em-vigor/pdf)
- **Rotativo to parcelamento does not reset the cap.** Res. 5.112 takes the initial rotativo amount as the original value and counts juros/encargos from the first day of the rotativo. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/dicas-e-direitos/teto-de-juros-do-cartao-de-credito-conheca-mudancas-nos-limites-do-rotativo)
- Banks must show, on each fatura or statement, the original value of each operation and the total juros/encargos charged so far. — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/dicas-e-direitos/teto-de-juros-do-cartao-de-credito-conheca-mudancas-nos-limites-do-rotativo)
- The cap does not apply to cartão consignado (fatura paid by payroll deduction). — [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/regra-que-limita-juro-do-cartao-a-100-da-divida-entra-em-vigor/)
- The cap applies only to debts contracted from January 2024. Start date: 3 Jan 2024 per [O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2024/01/03/amp/juros-do-rotativo-novo-limite-entra-em-vigor-nesta-quarta-entenda.html) and [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/regra-que-limita-juro-do-cartao-a-100-da-divida-entra-em-vigor/); some outlets (Agência Brasil, cited via search) say 2 Jan.
- Res. CMN 4.549/2017 cut rotativo use from unlimited to 30 days (until the next fatura). — [Idec PDF](https://idec.org.br/sites/default/files/contribuicoes_sobre_oferta_de_credito_e_endividamento_da_populacao.pdf) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- Because the cap is counted per operation, a new purchase on the same card is a new operation. If it goes unpaid, it would start its own rotativo with its own cap. The bank can keep charging normal rates on it. Treat this as an inference from the per-operation wording, not as a quoted rule.
- Once the cap is reached, further juros/encargos on that operation cannot be charged, but the principal plus up to 100% stays owed. Late-payment consequences such as negativação are separate from the cap.
- Game rule: debt(t) ≤ 2 × principal + IOF. IOF is charged on top.

### Gaps
- **Minimum payment %**: **[MEMORY]** the regulatory 15% minimum (BCB Circular 3.512/2010, raised to 15% in 2011) was revoked in 2017-2018. Each issuer now sets its own minimum, and many charge about 15% or more. Not re-confirmed this pass, because the BCB FAQ page would not render.
- **Rotativo, then mandatory instalment offer**: **[MEMORY]** under Res. 4.549/2017, if the fatura is not paid in full by the next due date, the issuer must offer a parcelamento at a lower rate than rotativo, or demand full payment. Res. 4.655/2018 made "regular" and "irregular" rotativo carry the same rate. Only the 30-day limit itself was confirmed (via Idec). Whether these rules still apply exactly this way in 2026 was not checked against the BCB text.
- No 2026 change to Res. 5.112 was found, and none was searched for specifically.

## 2. Minimum age and first limits for a credit card

### Takeaway
Since September 2025, Nubank offers 16-17-year-olds their **own** credit card. Its limit equals the money saved in a Caixinha, so it works as a secured card. Under 16, a minor can have an **additional card** on a parent's account, with a cap the parent sets. This pass found no confirmed figures for first limits at Nubank, Inter, PicPay, C6 or Mercado Pago.

### Cited Findings
- Nubank: 16- and 17-year-olds can request their own credit card in the app. It is issued in the teen's name, the limit is defined by the amount saved in the Caixinha, and it has "automatic protection against indebtedness". — [Nubank newsroom](https://nu.com/en/newsroom/consumers/nubank-launches-credit-card-for-u18-with-limit-defined-by-amount-saved-in-caixinha); [Nubank product page](https://nubank.com.br/nu/conta-menores/cartao-credito); [Portal Tela, 2025-09-28](https://www.portaltela.com/economia/2025/09/28/adolescentes-poderao-ter-cartao-de-credito-no-nubank/)
- An additional card in the minor's name, with a maximum set by the account holder, requires the minor's account and a request from the legal guardian who opened the account. — [Nubank blog](https://blog.nubank.com.br/cartao-de-credito-menor-de-18-anos/)
- Conflict: an older Nubank page for minors' accounts says the minimum age for a card with the credit function is 18. That predates the September 2025 launch. — [Nubank conta menores](https://nubank.com.br/nu/conta-menores)

### Inferences
- In the game, a 16-17 player can model a secured card: limit = Caixinha balance, which builds history. A 15-and-under player can only get a parent's additional card.

### Gaps
- Typical first limits for a young adult with no history (R$50-R$500) were **not verified** at any of the five issuers. Issuers don't publish them.
- Secured or "limite garantido" products at Inter (CDB-backed), PicPay, C6 (CDB as limit) and Mercado Pago were **[MEMORY]** only, not verified.

## 3. IOF on credit for individuals (2026)

### Takeaway
Most sources give individuals **0.0082% per day plus a fixed 0.38%**, with the daily part capped at 365 days (about 3%). The 2025 decrees (12.466, 12.467 and 12.499/2025) mainly raised the **PJ** daily rate (0.0041% to 0.0082%). The STF (Moraes, 16 Jul 2025, ADC 96) upheld them except on risco sacado. One law firm lists PF at 0.0041%/day, which looks wrong (conflict below).

### Cited Findings
- Daily rate for PF of 0.0082% on the principal, with a cap of about 3% a year (2026 guides). — [ecarts IOF 2026](https://ecarts.com.br/financas/iof-2026-aliquotas-oficiais-calculo-e-como-funciona/); [sum.money calculator](https://sum.money/br/iof/)
- Additional 0.38% regardless of term, for PF or PJ (Decreto 6.306/2007 §15). The daily part has a maximum of 3% for operations longer than 365 days. — [Planalto Decreto 6.339/2008](https://planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2007-2010/2008/decreto/d6339.htm) (via search summary)
- 2025: PJ daily rate raised from 0.0041% to 0.0082%, with the 0.38% fixed rate kept. Moraes restored the decree on 16 Jul 2025 except for risco sacado; the ruling was an injunction awaiting the plenary. — [Demarest](https://www.demarest.com.br/majoracao-do-iof-stf-publica-decisao-cautelar-restabelecendo-a-eficacia-do-decreto-no-12-499-2025/); [Forbes Brasil, Jul 2025](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2025/07/como-ficam-as-novas-aliquotas-do-iof-apos-decisao-de-alexandre-de-moraes/)
- **Conflict**: [Barbieri Advogados](https://www.barbieriadvogados.com/?p=4066) lists PF at 0.0041%/day for 2025, while other coverage says PF credit IOF did not change in 2025. — [Nord Investimentos](https://www.nordinvestimentos.com.br/blog/iof-novas-aliquotas-depois-decisao-moraes/)

### Inferences
- **[MEMORY]** PF has been 0.0082%/day + 0.38% since Decreto 6.339 (January 2008), and 0.0041% was the PJ rate before 2025. So the Barbieri figure probably mislabels PJ as PF. The writer should present 0.0082% + 0.38% as the PF rate and note the conflict.
- Game approximation: IOF ≈ 0.38% + 0.0082% × days, capped at about 3.38% total. It applies to personal loans and, **[MEMORY]**, to rotativo and fatura instalments, but not to on-time card purchases.

### Gaps
- No primary Receita Federal page was fetched. No 2026 change was found (absence not proven). The STF plenary's merits ruling was not checked.

## 6. Cheque especial

### Takeaway
The cap is 8% a month for individuals, in force since 6 Jan 2020 (approved by the CMN in Nov 2019). That is about 151.8% a year compounded; one source's "129%" figure looks wrong. A blog citing BCB data gives an average of **141.1% a year in April 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- Cap of 8% a month for PF, approved Nov 2019, in force 6 Jan 2020. — [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/governo-limita-juro-do-cheque-especial-a-8-ao-mes/); [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/economia/limite-de-juros-para-cheque-especial-comeca-a-valer/)
- Average cheque-especial rate of 141.1% a year in April 2026, "according to BCB data" (a blog, not the BCB itself). — [Guardar Dinheiro](https://guardardinheiro.com.br/blog/cheque-especial-taxa-juros-2026)
- After the cap, the average fell to 165.5% a year (2020). — [Suno](https://www.suno.com.br/noticias/?p=54393)
- The effective cost can exceed 8% because IOF is added on top. — [Suno](https://www.suno.com.br/noticias/juros-cheque-especial-limite-bc/amp/)
- The R$500 rule: banks were allowed to charge a fee for limits above R$500. Four big banks postponed charging it. — [InvestNews](https://investnews.com.br/economia/bancos-ja-podem-cobrar-taxa-pelo-cheque-especial-veja-perguntas-e-respostas/amp/); [Bem Paraná](https://www.bemparana.com.br/noticia/quatro-dos-maiores-bancos-adiam-cobranca-de-taxa-do-cheque-especial)

### Inferences
- 1.08^12 − 1 = 151.8% a year is the arithmetic of the cap. An April 2026 average of 141% a year (about 7.6% a month) means rates cluster near the cap.

### Gaps
- **[MEMORY]** The fee was up to 0.25% a month on the limit above R$500, set by Res. 4.765/2019. The OAB challenged it and the STF suspended it in 2020 (ADI 6407). Its 2026 status was not verified.
- No 2024 or 2025 averages were fetched from the BCB series.

## 7. Tesouro Direto practical details

### Takeaway
Confirmed: a B3 custody fee of **0.20% a year** on Tesouro Selic, Prefixado and IPCA+. **Tesouro Selic is exempt on the first R$10,000 per CPF.** Since 31 Dec 2024, the fee is charged on events (sale, maturity, coupon) rather than every six months. Not confirmed: the minimum ticket and Oct-Nov 2026 rates.

### Cited Findings
- 0.20% a year B3 custody fee, accrued daily, on Prefixado, Selic and IPCA+. — [Tesouro Direto: nova taxa de custódia](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/en/b/entenda-a-nova-taxa-de-custodia); [B3 OC 137/2023](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/B1/D0/95/FA/3F1B98109EBF7498DC0D8AA8/OC%20137-2023-PRE%20Pol%C3%ADtica%20de%20Tarifa%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Tesouro%20Direto_Educa_%20(PT).pdf)
- Tesouro Selic is exempt up to R$10,000 per CPF; only the excess is charged (e.g. on a R$10,100 balance, the fee applies to R$100). — [Rico](https://riconnect.rico.com.vc/?p=179971); [Tesouro Direto regras](https://www.tesourodireto.com.br/conheca/regras.htm)
- From 31 Dec 2024 the fee is no longer charged semi-annually; it is charged on redemption, maturity or coupon payment. — [B3 OC 014/2024](https://b3.com.br/data/files/CC/A7/6E/11/CC543910B371F339AC094EA8/OC%20014-2024-VPC%20Pol%C3%ADtica%20de%20tarifa%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Tesouro%20Direto_cobranca_semestral.pdf); [Bora Investir (B3)](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/tipos-de-investimentos/renda-fixa/tesouro-direto/taxa-de-custodia-do-tesouro-direto-deixa-de-ser-cobrada-semestralmente/)

## 8. Poupança figures

### Takeaway
Poupança returned **about 8.19-8.26% in 2025** (sources differ slightly). The 12-month figure to May-June 2026 is **about 8.32%**, and year to date about 3.35% (to May) or "4%" (around June). No figures after June 2026 were found.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 total: 8.19%. — [Creditas](https://www.creditas.com/exponencial/rendimento-da-poupanca/). 2025 total (Dec 2025, 12 months): 8.2634%. — [VRI Consulting](https://www.vriconsulting.com.br/indices/poupanca.php). **Small conflict.**

### Inferences
- A monthly 0.6695% means TR ≈ 0.17% a month in May 2026, since 0.6695 ≈ (1.005 × (1 + TR)) − 1. TR is **not** near zero. One third-party source's claim that "TR in 2026 is near zero, so about 6.17% a year" conflicts with the VRI data and should be disregarded.
- **[MEMORY]** Under the Selic ≤ 8.5% rule, the return is 70% of Selic + TR. The rule dates from 2012, for deposits since 4 May 2012.

### Gaps
- No data after June 2026 (July to November) and no official BCB series fetched. The current TR level was only inferred.
