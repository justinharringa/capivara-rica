# Money life of Brazilian 10-17 year olds (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: this file covers mesada, spending, games, accounts for minors, the first phone, first work, government transfers, risks and literacy. Adult wages, credit and investing are in other notes. Pé-de-Meia amounts and Jovem Aprendiz pay are already in `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md` and `saving_investing.md`, so they are only confirmed or extended here. Every figure carries a date. "[≤2025]" marks data whose latest confirmable edition is 2025 or earlier. "[aggregator]" marks a secondary or commercial source. "[memory]" marks anything not re-verified in this session.

## 1. Allowance (mesada): how common, how much, how often

### Takeaway
About 4-6 in 10 Brazilian parents pay a mesada. It is small: roughly R$ 60-100 a month, paid monthly, mostly in cash, and spent mainly on school snacks. Among parents who don't pay one, the largest groups say money should be earned (35%) or prefer to "give when needed" (32%), so "money on demand" is a real, common model. No 2025-2026 national survey of amounts by age or class was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Serasa/Opinion Box, Oct 2023 ("Finanças para os Filhos: Dinheiro é Coisa de Adulto?")** [≤2025]:
  - **39%** of Brazilian parents give a mesada and **61%** do not.
  - **74%** of payers give **up to R$ 100**, and **53%** give **up to R$ 60**.
  - Payment is **monthly** for **62%** of payers. No weekly figure was published.
  - By child age, mesada-giving parents are **54%** for children 6-11 and **45%** for 15-18.
  - Main uses: buying **school snacks (33%)** and "learning to save" (32%).
  - **72%** of parents save or invest nothing for their children. **72%** know no financial product for children or teens. Products recalled: Inter Conta Digital Kids (8%), Caixa Cartão Adicional Mesada (5%), Next Nextjoy (4%).
  - In classes D/E, **64%** don't recall talking about money with their own parents. No sample size was published.
  - — [Serasa imprensa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/mesada-aos-filhos/); [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/seu-bolso/meu-dinheiro/39-dos-pais-brasileiros-tem-o-habito-de-dar-mesada-aos-filhos-diz-pesquisa/)
- **Serasa/Opinion Box, published 9 Oct 2024** [≤2025]:
  - **56%** of families pay a mesada, and **89%** say it builds money awareness.
  - **61%** of recipients get **up to R$ 100**. Amount depends on **behaviour at home and school performance**, so the performance-based mesada is the documented norm.
  - Most children start **before age 8**.
  - Payment form: **more than half in cash**, **23%** into a digital kids' account, **16%** on the child's own debit card.
  - Main use: **snacks at school**.
  - Why non-payers don't pay: **35%** "money must be earned through work", **32%** prefer to give money when needed, **27%** can't afford it.
  - — [CNN Brasil, 9 Oct 2024](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/economia/financas/56-das-familias-brasileiras-pagam-mesadas-para-filhos-mostra-pesquisa/)
- **Kidscorp Kids & Teens Tracking, 2025-2026**: **64%** of Brazilian teens aged 13-18 who play games receive a mesada, alone or alongside purchases made by their parents. In the last month, **clothes/accessories** and **eating out** tied as the top spending categories at **40%** each. — reported via search summary of [Meio & Mensagem, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/adolescentes-ampliam-presenca-no-consumo-online). The fetched article text did **not** contain the 64% or the 40% figures, so treat both as unverified. The sample is only 305 teens who game.
- **Nubank minors' accounts, Aug 2025**: average monthly receipts are **R$ 637** per minor account, and **55%** of that comes from women. Account holders are 13-15 (**43%**), 16-17 (**40%**) and 6-12 (**17%**). **76%** of guardians who open the accounts are women. — [Forbes Brasil, 29 Sep 2025](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2025/09/adeptos-ao-pix-para-troca-de-mensagens-publico-menor-de-idade-podera-ter-cartao-de-credito-proprio-no-nubank/). This is a self-selected, banked and likely higher-income population, so R$ 637 is far above the survey mesada. It probably includes gifts, Pix from relatives and teen earnings.
- IBGE household budgets: "doação e mesada" is about 1% of household income composition. — see `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md` (IBGE informativo 2025).

### Inferences
- A defensible game default for 2026: a mesada of **R$ 50-100/month** at age 10-14, rising toward R$ 100-150 at 15-17. Survey amounts are 2023-2024; one or two years of ~4-5% inflation adds little. The 15-17 band is an inference: no age-graded amounts were found.
- Three household "allowance models" fit the evidence and could be a starting-family variable: (a) fixed monthly mesada (~40-56% of families); (b) "pede quando precisa" on demand (~32% of non-payers); (c) "tem que trabalhar pra ganhar" (~35% of non-payers), which maps to the 14+ Jovem Aprendiz or informal-work path. A fourth, "can't afford it" (27% of non-payers), maps to low-income/Bolsa Família starts.
- Performance-linked pay (behaviour and grades) is the documented norm, which suits a game: good grades or chores could raise the mesada, and fights could cut it.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 national mesada survey was found from SPC/CNDL, Febraban, Mobills or Dinheirama. Searches for 2025 returned only the Serasa 2023/2024 waves and a Portuguese (EUR) article.
- No breakdown of mesada **amount by age** or **by social class** was found.
- No figure was found for weekly vs monthly beyond "62% monthly" (2023), or for chore-based ("mesada por tarefas") prevalence beyond "depends on behaviour/grades".
- No survey was found on **how many 10-17s contribute to household expenses**. The closest proxy is child labour (section 5).

## 2. What they spend on: snacks, games, online shopping, ads

### Takeaway
Snacks are the documented #1 use of a mesada. In games, **21% of internet users aged 11-17 spent money** (TIC Kids Online 2025), rising with age from 16% to 24%. Free Fire diamonds start at **R$ 3 (Pix)**, and 400 Robux cost about R$ 25. Teens are also heavily exposed to marketing: **53% of 11-17 users saw betting-game promotion** in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **TIC Kids Online Brasil 2025** (Cetic.br; fieldwork Mar-Aug 2025; 2,370 children/teens + 2,370 guardians; face-to-face):
  - **21%** of internet users aged 11-17 bought something or spent money to advance a level or get new items in online games.
  - By age: **11-12: 16%**, **13-14: 22%**, **15-17: 24%**.
  - — [search summary of Cetic.br material](https://cetic.br/en/tics/kidsonline/2025/criancas/E1/expandido/). The age split comes from a Cetic presentation re-hosted by [TJGO](https://www.tjgo.jus.br/files/2026/03%20-%20Marco/Pesquisa%20TIC_Kids_online_brasil_2025.pdf) and was not cross-checked against the indicator table.
- **TIC Kids Online 2025, executive summary** (fetched and read):
  - Among 11-17 users, **55%** saw ads on social networks, **52%** on TV, **52%** on video sites and **26%** on game sites. Social-network ad contact is **64% at 15-17** vs **42% at 11-12**.
  - **84%** saw product/brand content not labelled as advertising: unboxing **66%**, "how to use a product" **65%**, products a brand gave them **61%**.
  - **53%** reported contact with **promotion of betting games ("jogos de apostas")**, measured for the first time.
  - **65%** of 11-17s agree that searching or talking about a product online increases the ads they get for it.
  - **52%** watch videos of people playing video games.
  - — [Cetic.br, TIC Kids Online 2025 resumo executivo (PDF, Mar 2026)](https://cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/pt-br/20260317181001/tic_kids_online_2025_resumo_executivo.pdf.pdf)
- **Kidscorp U18 Radar / Kids & Teens Tracking (2025-2026, Brazilian teens 13-18)**:
  - **60%** already buy online.
  - **54%** of teens who game make in-game or in-app purchases (n=305 gamers).
  - Preferred payment: **debit card 42%**, **cash 31%**.
  - Favourite games: **Roblox 48.2%**, **Minecraft 45.8%**. **53%** of gamers play mainly on the phone.

  - — [Meio & Mensagem, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/adolescentes-ampliam-presenca-no-consumo-online). The 54% is not comparable with TIC's 21%: the base is gamers only, the sample is small and it is a commercial panel.

  - **400 Robux ≈ R$ 23-27** depending on the platform (May 2026 estimate).

### Inferences
- A game "lanche" price of **R$ 8-15** (salgado + drink at a school cantina) is plausible but **[memory/unverified]**: no 2026 source was found. At R$ 10/school day × ~20 days, the daily lanche (R$ 200/month) would exceed a typical R$ 60-100 mesada. That is a clean opening dilemma: lanche every day vs bring from home and save.
- A plausible in-game spending price ladder: R$ 3 (smallest diamond pack) → R$ 20-25 (a battle pass or 400 Robux) → R$ 50 (gift card) → R$ 210 (5,600 diamonds). The **R$ 3** micro-purchase is the key "small amounts add up" teaching moment.
- The "Pix as message" behaviour (R$ 0,01-1 Pix with text) is a very Brazilian, meme-able detail for the narrator.

### Gaps
- TIC Kids Online **2024** in-game spending figure: not retrieved. The 2025 edition was used.
- No reliable 2025-2026 data was found on teen spending on **streaming, sneakers, transport, hair/beauty or phone recarga**, or on the average monthly in-game spend in BRL.
- EA FC points prices were not looked up.

## 3. Saving and accounts: what a minor can legally hold and do

### Takeaway
Minors can hold a bank account, a debit card and Pix, but only an account **opened by a legal guardian**, who sees everything. Credit cards for under-18s are normally only **additional cards on the guardian's account**. The one exception found: in Sept 2025 Nubank began a gradual rollout of a **secured credit card for 16-17 year olds**, with the limit backed by their own Caixinha savings. So "minimum age 18 for credit" is the rule for a card **in one's own name with unsecured credit**, but it is not absolute. Pé-de-Meia gives millions of low-income 14+ students an automatic Caixa Tem poupança, which is often their first account.

### Cited Findings
- **Pix has no minimum age set by the BCB**. A minor needs an account at an institution that offers Pix, and a guardian must create that account. — [Serasa blog](https://www.serasa.com.br/blog/com-quantos-anos-pode-ter-pix/); [Inter blog](https://blog.inter.co/com-quantos-anos-pode-ter-pix/); [meutudo](https://meutudo.com.br/blog/com-quantos-anos-pode-ter-pix/) [aggregators]
- **Nubank minor account (as described 3 Jan 2026)**:
  - Ages **6-17**. The guardian opens it, and only the adult who created it sees the control panel: balance, debit spending, Pix and Caixinhas, in real time.
  - The minor can send and receive Pix and TED, use a **debit** card (online, in person, and Banco24Horas withdrawals), buy phone **recarga**, use "Modo Rua", and buy **gift cards** at a discount.
  - Caixinhas (RDB) yield **100% of CDI**.
  - The article says "no access to credit".
  - — [Estado de Minas, 3 Jan 2026](https://www.em.com.br/emfoco/2026/01/03/nubank-libera-conta-para-menores-de-idade-com-pix-cartao-e-controle-dos-pais-direto-no-app/). Nubank's own blog also states 6-17 (per search summary): [Nubank blog](https://blog.nubank.com.br/conta-menor-de-18/). Older articles cite 12+ (Tecnoblog launch-era) or 10+: [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/atras-de-c6-next-e-inter-nubank-libera-conta-para-menores-de-18-anos/); [Dinheiro Experto](https://dinheiroexperto.com.br/nubank-aceita-menor/).
- **Nubank credit card for 16-17s (announced Sept 2025)**:
  - Teens **16-17** can request it **without parental authorisation**. Rollout is gradual, and guardians are notified.
  - The **limit equals the money the teen keeps in Caixinhas** (secured), so Nubank takes no credit risk. The card has its own fatura, which the guardian can see via Parental Control, and it comes with "educational missions".
  - **47%** of minors have active Caixinhas vs **28%** of adults.
  - — [Forbes Brasil, 29 Sep 2025](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-money/2025/09/adeptos-ao-pix-para-troca-de-mensagens-publico-menor-de-idade-podera-ter-cartao-de-credito-proprio-no-nubank/)
  - **Conflict:** Estado de Minas (Jan 2026) says the minor account has "no access to credit". It may be describing the base account only. Whether the 16-17 card reached all users by Nov 2026 is unconfirmed.
- **Inter (Conta Kids/teen) and C6 (Konta Jovem)**: listed as **12-17**, with a virtual debit card and optional physical card. — [eCarts comparison 2026](https://ecarts.com.br/financas/conta-digital-para-adolescente-em-2026-como-abrir-limites-e-7-bancos/) [aggregator]; [Serasa, bancos que abrem conta para menores](https://www.serasa.com.br/credito/blog/bancos-que-abrem-conta-para-menores-de-idade/)
  - Inter lets under-18s enable a credit function by investing in "CDB Mais Limite" (a secured card). — [search summary of Inter/iDinheiro blogs](https://blog.inter.co/cartao-de-credito-para-menor-de-18-anos/) [aggregator]
- **Credit card rule**: a credit card in one's own name is for 18+. For minors the standard route is an **additional card** on a guardian's account with a shared limit, where spending falls on the guardian. Each bank sets its own minimum age for additional cards. Blogs cite Itaú/Bradesco 12, Nubank 10 and PAN 16, and these figures conflict across sites. — [Serasa, cartão para menor de idade](https://www.serasa.com.br/credito/blog/cartao-para-menor-idade/); [iDinheiro](https://www.idinheiro.com.br/cartao-de-credito/cartao-de-credito-para-menor-de-idade/); [Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/posso-ter-um-cartao-de-credito-sendo-menor-de-idade/) [aggregators]
  - Itaú "Click" additional card lets the guardian set a custom limit that can "work as a mesada". — same sources
- **Caixa** offered a "Cartão Adicional Mesada", recalled by 5% of parents in Oct 2023. — [Serasa imprensa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/mesada-aos-filhos/)
- **Pé-de-Meia** pays into a **Caixa Tem poupança opened automatically in the student's name** (ages 14-24, public ensino médio, CadÚnico). This is documented in `saving_investing.md` ([SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)).
- **Minors can hold Tesouro Direto on their own CPF**; see `saving_investing.md`.
- Parents' saving for children (Oct 2023): **72%** don't save for them, and of those who do, **50%** use their own account. — [Serasa imprensa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/mesada-aos-filhos/)

### Inferences
- Account timeline for the game:
  - **Age 10**: cofrinho (cash) or a parent-opened digital account with debit card + Pix (Nubank 6+; most others 12+).
  - **Age 12-14**: a teen account at most digital banks.
  - **Age 14+**: Pé-de-Meia poupança, if low-income and in public high school.
  - **Age 16-17**: optional **secured** credit card (a limit equal to your own savings). This is a great teaching device: "your limit is your own money".
  - **Age 18**: real unsecured credit.
- "Using a parent's card" is realistic because additional cards for 10-12 year olds exist. The bill hits the parent, which is a natural family-conflict event.
- A guardian sees every transaction on a minor account, so "hiding a purchase" is only possible with cash, gift cards or a friend's account.

### Gaps
- No figure was found for the **share of 10-17s with any bank account**. I found no BCB or TIC indicator for it. The Nubank age mix (Aug 2025) gives no absolute count.
- Official per-bank rules for Itaú, Bradesco, Santander, BB, Caixa, PicPay (minor) and Mercado Pago were not verified on bank sites. C6 "Yellow" was not confirmed as a current product name.
- The BCB's normative text on minors' accounts (e.g. the Res. CMN on account opening) was not retrieved. The guardian-opens rule comes from bank/aggregator sources and is consistent with the Civil Code incapacity rules **[memory]**: under 16 "absolutamente incapaz", 16-17 "relativamente incapaz".
- No data was found on cofrinho prevalence.

## 4. First phone

### Takeaway
Brazilian children who own a smartphone got their first one at **10 years 3 months on average** (parents' survey, Jul 2025). Ownership is **75% at 10-12** and **90% at 13-16**, and is falling among under-12s in 2024-2025. In 2026 a good entry/intermediate Android costs about **R$ 850-1,700** (Galaxy A16/A17/A26, Moto G35/G56, Redmi Note 15). Phone theft is the most frequent documented shock.

### Cited Findings
- **Panorama Mobile Time/Opinion Box** (2,005 smartphone-owning parents of 0-16 year olds, 3-28 Jul 2025):
  - First smartphone at **10 years 3 months** on average, among children who have one.
  - Own-phone ownership: **0-3: 7%**, **10-12: 75%**, **13-16: 90%**.
  - Ownership fell in almost every band up to 12. The largest drop was at ages 4-6, from **30% to 17%**. The survey links this to the debate on early access and the **federal law banning phones in schools**.
  - — [Mobile Time, 5 Sep 2025](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/05/09/2025/criancas-smartphone-25/); [Jovem Pan](https://jovempan.com.br/brasil/braisl-registra-queda-inedita-no-numero-de-criancas-com-celular-proprio/)
- **TIC Kids Online 2025** (Mar-Aug 2025):
  - Own-phone ownership among internet users: **9-10: 55%** (67% in 2024), **11-12: 69%** (79% in 2024), **13-14: 78%**, **15-17: 95%**.
  - Mobile-phone internet use reaches **98%** at 15-17 and **82%** at 9-10, with no significant class difference.
  - **92%** of 9-17s were internet users in 2025, about **24 million**.
  - **28%** first went online by age 6.
  - Profiles: **66%** have their own WhatsApp and Instagram, **57%** TikTok, **50%** YouTube.
  - — [Cetic.br resumo executivo (PDF)](https://cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/pt-br/20260317181001/tic_kids_online_2025_resumo_executivo.pdf.pdf)
- **Kaspersky** (parents in six Latin American countries, classes A-C, undated): **73%** of children get their first smartphone or tablet before 10. — [Hardware.com.br](https://www.hardware.com.br/noticias/sete-a-cada-dez-criancas-brasileiras-tem-o-proprio-celular-antes-dos-10-anos/). The base includes tablets and different countries.
- **2026 entry/intermediate Android prices** (editorial buying guides, Mar-Oct 2026):
  - Galaxy A17 **from R$ 849** (Sept 2026).
  - Redmi Note 15 about **R$ 1,366** on Amazon.
  - Galaxy A26 about **R$ 1,500-1,700**.
  - Galaxy A16 5G and Moto G35 5G are cited as budget picks, and the Galaxy A16 is noted for 6 years of updates.
  - Moto G56 (8 GB/256 GB) is cited as a best-value pick.
  - The Galaxy A36 counts as an under-R$ 1,500 buy only on promotion.
  - — [TechTudo, Sept 2026](https://www.techtudo.com.br/listas/2026/09/melhor-celular-ate-r-1500-para-comprar-em-setembro-de-2026-edqualcomprarmb.ghtml); [Canaltech, Mar 2026](https://canaltech.com.br/smartphone/qual-e-o-melhor-celular-ate-r-1500-para-comprar-em-2026/); [Buscapé](https://www.buscape.com.br/celular/conteudo/melhor-celular-ate-1500-reais)
- **Phone theft**: São Paulo city lost **161,666 phones in 2025** (~442/day). — see `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md`.

### Inferences
- A natural game milestone is **"first phone" around 10-12**, often a parent's hand-me-down or a ~R$ 900 model. An intermediate phone (R$ 1,300-1,700) is a 14-17 goal.
- Price ladder for a "buy a phone" decision in 2026:
  - Entry: **R$ 850-1,000**.
  - Mid: **R$ 1,300-1,700**.
  - Aspirational: iPhone/Galaxy S, not looked up, but several thousand reais **[memory]**.
- A typical "12x sem juros" on a parent's card is the obvious first parcelamento. At R$ 1,500 / 12 = R$ 125/month, that already exceeds a typical R$ 60-100 mesada, so the teen can't pay it alone without a job. That is a good tension for the 14 start.
- Optional add-ons: capinha/película (screen protector) and seguro. Theft is frequent, so insurance is a meaningful decision in a SP-like city.

### Gaps
- No source was retrieved on **how** first phones are bought: parcelado on a parent's card vs carnê/boleto vs gift. Prices of película, capinha and phone insurance for budget models were not retrieved.
- No teen-specific phone theft victimisation rate was found.
- Phone prices are editorial-guide snapshots, not live Magalu/Casas Bahia/Mercado Livre pages.

## 5. First work: Jovem Aprendiz, the under-16 rule and informal work

### Takeaway
Confirmed: the **Constitution (art. 7, XXXIII) bans any work under 16, except as an apprentice from 14**, and bans night, dangerous or unhealthy work under 18. Apprentice **FGTS is 2%** (Lei 8.036/90 art. 15 §7, added by Lei 10.097/2000). Apprentice pay of R$ 737 (4 h/day) to R$ 1,105.50 (6 h/day) at the 2026 hourly minimum is consistent with what is already in the notes. Informal work is common in practice: **1.65 million** 5-17 year olds were in child labour in 2024, and **55.5%** of them were 16-17.

### Cited Findings
- **CF art. 7 XXXIII** (as amended by EC 20/1998):
  - No work under **16**, except as an **apprentice from 14**.
  - No night, dangerous or unhealthy work under **18**.
  - EC 20 raised the general minimum from 14 to 16.
  - — [Senado (legislation text)](https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento?dm=4712961); [Aurum, Lei da Aprendizagem](https://www.aurum.com.br/blog/lei-da-aprendizagem/)
- **CLT art. 403**, as rewritten by **Lei 10.097/2000**: "é proibido qualquer trabalho a menores de dezesseis anos de idade, salvo na condição de aprendiz, a partir dos quatorze anos". — [Senado](https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento?dm=4712961)
- **Apprentice contract terms**:
  - **FGTS 2%** of pay (Lei 8.036/90 art. 15 §7).
  - Fixed term of up to **2 years**.
  - Ages **14 to under 24**.
  - **6 h/day** cap (8 h only with ensino médio complete; see the other note).
  - — [Senado](https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento?dm=4712943); [Aurum](https://www.aurum.com.br/blog/lei-da-aprendizagem/). The 2% FGTS rate confirms the item marked "[unverified law]" in `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md`.
- **Pay**: R$ 737 (4 h) / R$ 1,105.50 (6 h) at R$ 7.37/h (2026). Already in `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md` ([Genyo](https://genyo.com.br/jovem-aprendiz/)) and consistent with the 2026 minimum wage of R$ 1,621. Not re-fetched here.
- **Child labour (IBGE PNAD Contínua, 2024 data released 19 Sep 2025)** [≤2025]:
  - **1.650 million** people aged 5-17 in child labour, **4.3%** of the 37.9 million in that age group.
  - That was **+2.1%** vs 2023 (1.616 million, the series low), but **-21.4%** since 2016.
  - **55.5%** were aged **16-17**.
  - Northeast **547k**, South **226k** (+13.6%).
  - About **560k** were in the worst forms (Lista TIP), the lowest in the series.
  - — [IBGE Agência de Notícias](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/pt/agencia-noticias/44541-pais-tinha-1-650-milhao-de-criancas-e-adolescentes-em-situacao-de-trabalho-infantil-em-2024.html?lang=pt-BR); [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-brasil/trabalho-infantil-volta-a-crescer-no-brasil-em-2024-diz-ibge/)

### Inferences
- Legal work options for the game:
  - **Under 14**: only family chores, odd jobs for neighbours or selling to friends (informal; legally grey to prohibited if it is "work").
  - **14-15**: Jovem Aprendiz only.
  - **16-17**: aprendiz, or a regular CLT job (daytime, non-hazardous) or estágio.
  - Hazardous gigs such as moto delivery are out under 18.
- Teen "bicos" that fit the tone without glamorising illegal labour: selling brigadeiro/bala at school, reselling (e.g. clothes or phone accessories), lawn or pet care, Free Fire "account boosting", and editing TikTok videos for others. All of these are **[memory/unsourced]** as prevalence claims.
- The 2% FGTS means an aprendiz on R$ 1,105.50 accrues about R$ 22/month in FGTS. That is a small but visible "money you can't touch yet" line, similar to Pé-de-Meia's locked R$ 1,000/year.

### Gaps
- No quantitative source was found on informal teen income: amounts from bicos, reselling, or Kwai/TikTok creator monetisation by minors (platform rules restrict monetisation to 18+ **[memory]**).
- No count was found of active Jovem Aprendiz contracts in 2026 (MTE publishes this; not retrieved).

## 6. Government money: Pé-de-Meia and Bolsa Família per child

### Takeaway
Pé-de-Meia (14-24, public ensino médio, CadÚnico) pays up to **R$ 9,200 over three years**. That is R$ 200 for enrolment, up to 9 × R$ 200 for ≥80% attendance, R$ 1,000/year locked until graduation, and R$ 200 for ENEM, all into an automatic **Caixa Tem poupança** (details in `saving_investing.md`). Bolsa Família adds **R$ 50/month per child or teen aged 7 to 18** (and R$ 150 per child 0-6) on top of a family floor of R$ 600. The R$ 50 is paid to the family, not the teen.

### Cited Findings
- Pé-de-Meia mechanics and the 2026 schedule are fully documented in `saving_investing.md` and `careers_wages_cost_of_living.md`. Key fact for the game: the R$ 1,000 "incentivo-conclusão" is **locked until the diploma**, and **attendance below 80%** forfeits that month's R$ 200. — [SouBrasília](https://soubrasilia.com/pe-de-meia-poupanca-estudante-ensino-medio/)
- **Bolsa Família (2026)**:
  - Minimum **R$ 600** per family.
  - **R$ 150** per child aged 0-6 (Benefício Primeira Infância).
  - **R$ 50** for ages 7 to 18 incomplete (Benefício Variável Familiar), plus pregnant and nursing women.
  - Eligibility: income up to **R$ 218 per person per month**, and registration in CadÚnico.
  - Worked example: 2 children aged 3 and 10 → R$ 600 + 150 + 50 = **R$ 800**.
  - — [Seu Crédito Digital, 2026](https://seucreditodigital.com.br/bolsa-familia-2026-extras-r-50-e-r-150/); [Concursos no Brasil, Apr 2026](https://concursosnobrasil.com/beneficios-sociais/2026/04/24/bolsa-familia-de-maio-veja-quem-recebe-o-valor-extra-de-r-150/) [aggregators; upper age bound reported as "7-17" by one outlet]. The 2023 relaunch with R$ 150 per child under 6: [Congresso em Foco](https://congressoemfoco.com.br/area/governo/lula-relanca-o-bolsa-familia-com-adicional-de-r-150-por-crianca-ate-seis-anos).
- Bolsa Família conditionalities: school attendance **[memory]**: 60% for ages 4-5 and 75% for 6-18. Not verified in this session.
- STF (Nov 2024) required the government to stop Bolsa Família/BPC money flowing to bets. — see `saving_investing.md`.

### Inferences
- For a low-income starting family at age 14-17, the teen "sees" government money in two ways. The **family** gets an extra R$ 50 on the Bolsa Família because of them. The **teen personally** gets Pé-de-Meia, about R$ 200/month plus locked lumps. Dropping out to work costs them roughly R$ 255/month on average **plus** the locked R$ 1,000/year, which is a clean trade-off versus a ~R$ 737-1,106 aprendiz wage. Both can be combined, since apprenticeship requires school attendance **[memory]**.

### Gaps
- No official gov.br/MDS page was fetched to confirm the 2026 Bolsa Família amounts and age cut-off. The R$ 600/150/50 structure has been stable since 2023 **[memory]**.
- I found no data on how teens experience or spend Pé-de-Meia money (e.g. share given to family vs kept).

## 7. Risks: bets, scams and a parent's card

### Takeaway
Betting is illegal under 18, yet **11% of 10-17s bet in 2025**, rising to **20% of boys aged 16-17** (Ipsos/Unico). A school survey found **20.4%** of students had ever bet, and **49.7%** in the 3rd year of ensino médio. **53%** of 11-17 internet users saw betting promotion in 2025. Teen-facing scams are dominated by fake **free Free Fire diamonds "por ID"**, which ask for passwords, APKs or an "advance fee" by Pix.

### Cited Findings
- **Ipsos for Unico** (1,200 youths aged 10-17, 21 Aug-1 Sep 2025):
  - **11%** bet on bets in 2025. **9%** in the last four months.
  - **Boys 16-17: 20%**, **girls 14-15: 14%**, **girls 10-13: 4%** (including "tigrinho").
  - Motives: curiosity **41%**, easy money **34%**, influencer/media **9%**.
  - Context: facial recognition has been mandatory on licensed platforms since **Jan 2025**, specifically to block use of a parent's CPF. **ECA Digital** took effect on **17 Mar 2026**.
  - — [BNLData](https://bnldata.com.br/apostas-online-11-dos-jovens-brasileiros-entre-10-e-17-anos-usaram-bets-em-2025/); [iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/br/apostas-esportivas/11-dos-menores-de-idade-realizam-apostas-online-no-brasil/)
- **Frente Parlamentar Mista da Educação + Equidade.info** (1,912 students in 191 schools, anos finais do fundamental and ensino médio; date of release uncertain, coverage 2025-2026):
  - **20.4%** had ever bet online.
  - **49.7%** in the 3rd year of ensino médio.
  - **9.5%** of students aged ≤11.
  - Boys **27.8%** vs girls **12.6%**.
  - Students reported losses of about R$ 1 billion (Fast Company headline).
  - — [Fast Company Brasil](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/impacto/bets-entre-estudantes-perdas-1-bilhao/); [Congresso em Foco](https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/122143/um-quinto-dos-jovens-de-ate-17-anos-ja-apostou-em-bet-revela-pesquisa). The R$ 1 bi figure is headline-level only and was not verified.
- A press report says youths give up mesada, lanche and even transport money for online bets (anecdotal). — [BNews/Agência Brasil](https://www.bnews.com.br/noticias/economia-e-mercado/jovens-caem-em-armadilha-das-apostas-line-e-perdem-mesada-lanche-e-ate-o-dinheiro-do-transporte.html)
- **Gen Z (16-29) in 2025**: 27% bet and 38% faced a scam (Anbima Raio X 2026). See `saving_investing.md`.
- **TIC Kids 2025**: **53%** of 11-17 users saw betting-game promotion, and **84%** saw unlabelled brand content. — [Cetic.br resumo executivo](https://cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/pt-br/20260317181001/tic_kids_online_2025_resumo_executivo.pdf.pdf)
- **Fake Free Fire diamonds**:
  - Garena has **no official free-diamonds-by-ID method**.
  - Red flags: exaggerated amounts (1,000 / 9,999), requests for password or verification code, APK installs, "advance fee via Pix", and offers like "100 diamonds for R$ 1".
  - The official channel named by Garena BR support is **recargajogo.com.br**.
  - — [Free Fire Mania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/diamantes-gratis-free-fire-por-id-funciona-ou-golpe.html); [Diário da Mídia](https://diariodamidia.com.br/free-fire-gerador-de-diamantes-pelo-id-e-golpe-e-expoe-sua-conta) [aggregators, 2026]
- **Fake job postings** that charge a Pix "taxa simbólica" for registration are a known pattern. The **Receita Federal** repeatedly denied any "taxa do Pix" (Jan 2025, again 2026) and called such messages scams. — [Diário de Pernambuco, Jan 2025](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/noticia/economia/2025/01/receita-alerta-para-golpes-com-taxa-falsa-sobre-pix.html); [Portal de Prefeitura, 5 Aug 2026](https://portaldeprefeitura.com.br/brasil/receita-federal-faz-alerta-para-golpes-com-taxa-falsa-sobre-pix/587147/)

### Inferences
- Event candidates with real grounding:
  - (a) a "diamantes grátis" link that steals the account or asks for a R$ 10 Pix fee;
  - (b) a classmate showing "tigrinho" winnings;
  - (c) a fake "vaga de jovem aprendiz" asking for a R$ 30-50 "taxa de inscrição" (amount **[memory/illustrative]**);
  - (d) an influencer "unboxing" a product the player then wants;
  - (e) using a parent's additional card for diamonds, which the parent sees on the fatura.
- Since betting is illegal for minors and the game targets minors, the bet event should show the trap (losses, "quase ganhei"), never a playable real-odds betting mechanic. The tech/legal notes already advise this.

### Gaps
- No quantitative 2025-2026 study was found on scams **aimed at minors** specifically: WhatsApp account theft, fake Pix refund, or pyramid/"renda extra" pitches on TikTok aimed at teens. Febraban and SaferNet would be the next sources to check.
- No data was found on prevalence of minors using a parent's card without permission.

## 8. Financial literacy at these ages

### Takeaway
Brazilian 15-year-olds scored **416 in PISA 2022 financial literacy** (OECD 498), with ~45% below baseline. This is in `saving_investing.md`. The BCB's Aprender Valor programme (fundamental and médio public schools) measured short-term gains in 2022, largest for initially low scorers. No item-level BCB scores were found. Parental money talk starts early: 80% of parents talk money with kids, and the main topics are prices and the value of saving.

### Cited Findings
- **Aprender Valor impact evaluation (BCB, published Nov 2024)**:
  - Entry test Apr-May 2022, exit test Oct-Nov 2022. **48,613** students were assessed and **25,420 (52.3%)** were matched across both tests.
  - Scores rose overall. Gains were largest for **initially low scorers** and in schools that used more of the programme's materials (teacher training, integrated school projects).
  - Programme reach: **~1.2 million** students enrolled (date unclear), about 100k education professionals and 17k schools in 2,300 municipalities.
  - — [BCB Série Cidadania Financeira nº 9](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/cidadaniafinanceira/documentos_cidadania/serie_cidadania/S%C3%A9rie_cidadania_financeira_n9.pdf); [BCB REB 2023 box](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/publicacoes/boxe_relatorio_de_economia_bancaria/reb2023b10p.pdf); [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/bc-registra-mais-de-1-milhao-de-estudantes-no-aprender-valor/)
  - A small qualitative study found that students didn't understand what the assessments were for, and teachers were overloaded. — [RSD Journal](https://rsdjournal.org/index.php/rsd/article/download/30347/26127/347596)
- **Parents' financial talk (Serasa, Oct 2023)**:
  - **80%** say they talk finances with their children. Of these, **24%** start by age 5 and **23%** at 6-8.
  - Main topics: what things cost and what can be bought (**41%**), and the value of saving (**40%**).
  - — [Serasa imprensa](https://www.serasa.com.br/imprensa/mesada-aos-filhos/)
- **Teens' sense of advertising**: **65%** of 11-17s understand that searching for a product increases ads for it. A basic grasp of ad-targeting is already widespread. — [Cetic.br resumo executivo](https://cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/pt-br/20260317181001/tic_kids_online_2025_resumo_executivo.pdf.pdf)
- PISA 2022 FL, BNCC (juros simples vs compostos in ensino médio Mathematics), MEC "Na Ponta do Lápis" and Aprender Valor's 2026 high-school expansion are covered in `saving_investing.md` and `games_landscape_and_evidence.md`.

### Inferences
- Age-appropriate content split implied by the materials:
  - **10-14**: price awareness, needs vs wants, saving toward a goal, ads and unboxing.
  - **15-17**: interest (simple vs compound, per BNCC), parcelamento, payslip/FGTS, bets and scams.
- Because gains were largest for low initial scorers, the game's 10-year-old phase should assume near-zero prior knowledge and teach through choices, not quizzes.

### Gaps
- No Aprender Valor proficiency levels or effect sizes by age band (10-14 vs 15-17) were retrieved.
- No PISA 2022 Brazil **item-level** findings were found.
- No ENEF/Serasa youth knowledge survey from 2025-2026 was found.
- No catalogue of money-related ENEM questions was retrieved.
